## Safety and responsibility

**21. Are the lessons and tests safe to use as written?**
> All content, drills, games, lesson plans and fitness tests are recommendations only. The teacher or user decides
> whether and how to use them and must adapt every activity — including fitness tests such as the 20 m shuttle
> run — to the pupils' age, health and ability and to the environment.

The products are for qualified teachers. You know your pupils, your space and your equipment. Warm up, supervise, allow
water breaks, and stop any pupil who reports pain, dizziness, chest discomfort or unusual shortness of breath. This is
not medical advice.

**22. Is the 20 m Shuttle Run an official or medical test?**
No. It is an independent tool that follows the protocol described by Léger et al. (1988). The VO₂max value is an
estimate, not a medical test or a diagnosis. It is a maximal-effort test: follow your school's policy on medical
clearance, and adapt it to your pupils. The adapted 15 m course is for younger pupils or pupils with disabilities; its
results are not comparable with the 20 m course and it gives no VO₂max estimate. No built-in reference thresholds are shipped; you can enter your own.

## Privacy and your data

**23. Does the software send my data anywhere?**
No. The tools run in your browser, need no account and make no network requests. They do not send us, or anyone, any
data. We cannot see what you type. (When you buy, {{MOR_NAME}} handles your payment and order details; see the Privacy
Policy: {{PRIVACY_URL}}.)

**24. Where is my data kept?**
Settings and your own entries are saved only in that browser's local storage, on that device. Lesson packs and the
activity pack are documents and store nothing. Data is not shared between devices or browsers. If you type pupils'
names, they stay on your device; bib numbers or initials are enough.

**25. How do I back up or delete my data?**
Each of the four tools has a button **"Delete all data on this device"**. It first offers to download a backup file
(JSON), then asks you to type a word before it erases only that tool's own data. A backup file can be loaded back with
"Restore from backup file". If you clear your browser's site data, the data of every tool in that browser is deleted too.
Nothing is ever sent to us, so we hold no copy and cannot restore or delete it for you.
