# Frequently asked questions — draft

These answers describe planned digital resources. Check the product page and delivered files before purchase. The teacher chooses and adapts all teaching activities.

## Can I use the resources without an internet connection?
Some standalone boards can run from an opened HTML file without a network connection. Availability and file requirements vary by product. Check the download instructions.

## Can I open the files on a phone or tablet?
Device compatibility has not yet been verified on physical devices for these standalone products. Ask support for the current tested formats.

## Will my work sync across devices?
Do not assume synchronization. Standalone board data may remain in the browser where it was entered. Export a copy where the product offers that option.

## What languages are offered?
The planned store includes English, Hebrew, Arabic, Russian and Spanish copy. Some translations are AI-produced drafts and may need proofreading.

## Can I share a download with another teacher?
The proposed Single-Teacher licence is for one named teacher. A proposed School licence covers eligible staff at one campus. Read the final licence before sharing: {{TERMS_URL}}.

## How do I request help or report an error?
Contact {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}} and include the product name, version if available, and a description of the issue. Do not send student-identifying information.

## How do refunds work?
Read the reviewed refund policy at {{REFUND_POLICY_URL}}. Send questions or requests to {{SUPPORT_EMAIL}}. Applicable statutory rights remain in place.

## Are the materials official standards or approved assessments?
No official approval is claimed. Teachers remain responsible for choosing suitable activities, safety precautions and adaptations.

## When will each product be available?
Availability has not been confirmed. See {{CATALOG_URL}} for the current status after the store is launched.
