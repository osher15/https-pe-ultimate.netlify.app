## Part B — the offline tools

**B1. No data leaves your device.** The tools run in your browser, need no account and make no network requests.
They do not send us, or anyone, any data. We cannot see what you type.

**B2. What is stored, and where.** Settings and your own entries are saved only in that browser's local storage
(`localStorage`) on that device, under the tool's own keys:

| Tool | Keys | What is stored |
|---|---|---|
| 20 m Shuttle Run (Beep Test) | `pe-shuttle.v1`, `pe-shuttle.v1.lang`; `pe-shuttle.v1.corrupt` only if saved data could not be read | settings, language, the runner list, results, your own thresholds |
| Interval Trainer | `pe-tools.interval-trainer.v1` | language, sound and voice, timer values, station settings and list, library filter |
| Scoreboard | `pe-scoreboard.v1`, `pe-scoreboard.lang.v1` | current match, team names, scores, last 20 results, language |
| Coach Board | `pe-ultimate-coach-board-v1` | boards, player numbers, labels and routes |

Lesson-plan packs are documents and store nothing.

**B3. Pupils' names.** You may type pupils' names or bib numbers (for example in the Shuttle Run or as labels).
They stay on your device. Use only what you need; bib numbers or initials are enough. *You* decide what you enter,
so follow your school's data-protection rules. Exported files (CSV, JSON, PNG) are saved where you choose and are
your responsibility.

**B4. How to delete.**
- **Shuttle Run:** "Delete all data on this device" (tap twice) removes exactly the keys above.
- **Other tools:** they have no delete-all button in the current version *[product gap — see
  open-questions.md; update this line when the button is added]*. Delete their data by clearing this site's data
  in your browser settings (for files opened from your device, clear the browser's site data for local files)
  *[add exact steps per browser]*.
- Clearing the browser's site data also deletes the data of every tool.
- Device or browser backups and sync (for example a phone backup) may keep copies outside our control. Nothing
  is ever sent to us, so we hold no copy and cannot delete it for you.

**B5. Cookies and consent.** The tools set no cookies. Their local storage holds only what is needed for the
function you asked for (keeping your settings and entries), so we consider it exempt from consent under the
ePrivacy Directive 2002/58/EC Art. 5(3) ("strictly necessary") *[lawyer to confirm, including UK PECR]*.
