# Safety Notice — texts to embed in products

Short texts for the About panel, footer or first page of each product. Keep them short; do not add medical
claims. These are guidance and a reminder of the teacher's responsibility; they do not exclude the seller's own
liability, which is dealt with only in the Terms of Sale (section 11).

## 1. General (lesson packs and drill/activity packs)

> **Safety.** These materials are for qualified teachers. You know your pupils, your space and your equipment:
> choose and adapt each activity to their age, fitness and needs, and supervise it. Check the area and
> equipment first, include a warm-up and cool-down, allow water breaks and take care in heat. Stop any pupil who reports pain, dizziness, chest discomfort or
> unusual shortness of breath. Follow your school's safety and medical policies. This is not medical advice.

## 2. 20 m Shuttle Run (Beep Test)

> **Safety.** This is a **maximal-effort** test. Before testing pupils with a known medical condition, follow
> your school's or local policy on medical clearance. Warm up first and cool down after, allow water and take care
> in heat, use a non-slip surface with clear run-off space, supervise throughout, and let pupils stop at any
> time. Stop a pupil at once if they report pain, dizziness, chest discomfort or unusual
> shortness of breath.
>
> **Adapted 15 m course.** For younger pupils or pupils with disabilities, so that they can complete the test
> at a good level and have a positive experience of success. Results are not comparable with the 20 m course,
> and no VO₂max estimate is given.
>
> The VO₂max value is an **estimate** based on Léger et al. (1988). It is not a medical test or a diagnosis.

## 3. Interval Trainer

> **Safety.** Choose exercises, levels and work/rest times that suit your pupils, and show correct technique
> first. Warm up and cool down, allow water breaks, take care in heat, supervise, and let pupils rest or stop at
> any time. Stop any pupil who reports pain or
> dizziness. Not medical advice.

## 4. Footer line (all tools, one line)

> Teaching aid for qualified teachers. Adapt to your pupils and supervise. Stop on pain or dizziness. Not medical
> advice.
