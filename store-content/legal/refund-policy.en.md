## 2. Faulty products (all countries)

If a product is faulty or not as described, you can ask us to fix or replace it. If that is not possible, or not
done within a reasonable time, you can get a refund (in full or in part). In the EU these rights come from the
Digital Content Directive (EU) 2019/770 as implemented in your country, and in the UK from the Consumer Rights
Act 2015. These rights do not depend on section 3 or 4 below.

## 3. Israel

Under the Consumer Protection Law 5741-1981, a consumer may in general cancel a distance sale within 14 days.

## 4. European Union and United Kingdom

**You have 14 days to withdraw** from a purchase of digital content (EU Consumer Rights Directive 2011/83/EU;
UK Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013).

You lose this right **only if all of the following happen**:
1. before the download starts, you tick a **separate, unticked checkbox** to ask for immediate access and to
   confirm that you then lose the right to withdraw (CRD Art. 16(m));
2. we (or {{MOR_NAME}}) send you a **confirmation of that request and acknowledgement on a durable medium**,
   such as the receipt e-mail (CRD Art. 8(7), as amended by Directive (EU) 2019/2161); and
3. the download has started.

Consent is never assumed from the purchase itself. **If you did not tick the box, or did not get the
confirmation, your 14-day right remains.** Sections 1 and 2 apply in every case.

## 5. United States and the rest of the world

Sections 1 and 2 apply, together with any mandatory consumer rights where you live.
