Orders are processed by **{{MOR_NAME}}** as merchant of record. Refunds are paid through {{MOR_NAME}}.

## 1. The general rule

Digital files cannot be returned. **Once you have downloaded or opened a product, the purchase is final** —
except in the cases in sections 2–4 and wherever the law gives you a right that cannot be taken away
(see sections 5–7).

## 2. Full refund

We refund the full price if:
- **(a) duplicate purchase:** you bought the same product with the same licence twice;
- **(b) not downloaded:** you have not downloaded or opened the product, and you ask within 14 days of purchase;
- **(c) billing error:** you were charged the wrong amount, or the charge was not made by you.

## 3. Faulty or not as described

If a product is faulty or does not match its description, we will first fix it or send you a corrected or
replacement file. If we cannot do this within a reasonable time ({{FIX_DAYS}} days), you get a full refund, or
a proportional partial refund if only part is affected (for example, one faulty item in a bundle).

## 4. Wrong licence

You can upgrade a Single-Teacher licence to a School licence by paying the difference. Changing from a School
licence to a Single-Teacher licence is not refunded, unless the law requires it.

## 5. European Union and United Kingdom — 14-day right of withdrawal

You have 14 days to withdraw from a purchase of digital content (EU Consumer Rights Directive 2011/83/EU; UK
Consumer Contracts (Information, Cancellation and Additional Charges) Regulations 2013). You lose this right
**only if all of the following happen**:
1. before the download starts, you tick a **separate, unticked checkbox** to ask for immediate access and to
   confirm that you then lose the right to withdraw (CRD Art. 16(m));
2. we (or {{MOR_NAME}}) send you a **confirmation of that request and acknowledgement on a durable medium**,
   such as the receipt e-mail (CRD Art. 8(7), as amended by Directive (EU) 2019/2161); and
3. the download has started.

Consent is never assumed from the purchase itself. **If you did not tick the box, or did not get the
confirmation, your 14-day right remains**, even after download. Your remedies for faulty content (Digital
Content Directive (EU) 2019/770; UK Consumer Rights Act 2015) always apply.

## 6. Israel

Under the Consumer Protection Law 5741-1981, a consumer may in general cancel a distance sale within 14 days.

## 7. United States and the rest of the world

Sections 1–4 apply, together with any mandatory consumer rights where you live.
