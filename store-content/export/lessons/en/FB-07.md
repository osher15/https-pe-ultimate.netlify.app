# 1. Lesson identity
**Name/code:** Attacking 2v1 Advantage; FB-07-EN
**Sport/subtopic:** Football; width, committing a defender, timed pass
**Age/experience:** 12–15; developing
**Duration/students:** 45 minutes; 18–30
**Group:** Girls, boys, or mixed; adapt by ability
**Equipment:** 8–12 balls, 28 cones, bibs, small goals
**Space/complexity:** field or gym; intermediate–advanced
# 2. Overall purpose
Develop use of a two-attacker advantage through width, carrying to commit one defender, and passing at the right moment to the free player.
# 3. Observable learning objectives
- Maintain 4–7 metres of width in 6/8 attacks.
- The ball carrier scans before the decision line.
- Pass when the defender commits or carry when the lane stays open in 5/7.
- The partner remains visible and ahead of the ball.
- Finish within ten seconds without contact.
# 4. Required prior knowledge
Passing, receiving on the move, finishing, and no-contact defence from Lessons 02–06. Students needing support use 2v0 with a cone defender.
# 5. Position in the learning pathway
- **Previous:** Football 06—close a passing lane.
- **Current:** It adds the numerical overload, asking two attackers to hold width, carry the ball until the defender commits, and release the pass at that exact moment; it withholds any recovery defender and any consequence for losing possession — the advantage lasts the whole attack — which Football 08 takes away.
- **Next:** Football 08—attack-to-defence transition after loss.
# 6. Lesson contribution
- **Motor:** carry and pass on the move.
- **Social:** trust.
- **Cognitive:** defender reading.
- **Tactical:** width, commit, free player.
- **Emotional:** patience under pressure.
# 7. Equipment and space
Four or five 8×18 metre corridors with a 2–3 metre end goal. **Setup:** 8 minutes; **pack-down:** 5. Three active; outside return.
# 8. Safety
- No tackle/contact.
- Defender 60%, then 75%.
- The ball stays down; restart after the finish clears.
- No entering another corridor; pass to a watching partner.
- Retrieve after scan; stop for crowding/pain.
# 9. Lesson sequence
## Opening
3 minutes. “Width makes the defender choose; that choice reveals the free player.” Purpose: width–commit–pass.
## Warm-up
7 minutes. Pairs in parallel lanes alternate pass and carry. A colour cue prompts pass or continuation. 3 minutes walking, 2 light run, 2 finish through gate.
## Main activity A
10 minutes. An observer points to a “closed” side; the carrier chooses pass or carry before the line. Rotate after four. **Cues:** controlled forward touch, partner ahead, lead pass. **Errors:** convergence, pass behind, eyes down, late choice.
## Main activity B
10 minutes. Central defender at 60%, no tackle; reach goal within ten seconds. Rotate every two. **Cues:** commit without collision, partner holds width, pass as the defender moves to the ball. **Errors:** predetermined action, carry at the defender, trailing partner, fast defender.
## Applied game
10 minutes. 2v1 with two end gates. Score for controlled dribble/pass; bonus if both attackers touch; safety point for no contact. **Variations:** shadow; 60%; 75% and touch limit.
## Closure
5 minutes. **Message:** “Pass when it creates advantage, not because it is required.” Demonstration, “I saw–I chose,” collection.
# 10. Common errors
- **Converge:** use coloured lanes.
- **Early pass:** reach the decision line.
- **Late pass:** two-second decision zone.
- **Partner behind:** stay half a step ahead.
- **Eyes down:** identify bib colour.
- **Carry into contact:** turn or pass.
# 11. Teaching points
- Width.
- Early scan.
- Commit without contact.
- Pass on commitment.
- Partner ahead.
- Lead pass.
- Decision before speed.
# 12. Adaptations
**Foundation:** 2v0/cone. **Intermediate:** 60%. **Advanced:** 75%, eight seconds, two-touch. **Large group:** five corridors. **Small group:** trio rotation. **Limited space:** 6×12. **Limited equipment:** cone goals. **Different needs:** walk, soft ball, wide route, visual/sound cue, fixed partner role.
# 13. Assessment
Width, scan, choice, timing, safety. **Success:** at least four of the five objectives achieved, each by its own stated measure. **Partial:** three objectives, or achieved against shadow only. **Not yet:** crowding, random choice, contact. Return to 2v0.
# 14. Reflection
**Teacher:** Was the defender fair and the lane wide? Student: What did the defender do before your choice? Improve: decision line and speed.
# 15. Continuity
**If achieved:** Football 08—transition after loss. **If not:** slow Main B; return to Football 05 for decision support.

# 19. Pedagogical value
- **Why important:** it turns passing from an instruction the teacher gives into a decision the defender forces, which is the moment students begin to play football rather than perform it.
- **Develops:** holding attacking width under pressure, carrying the ball with intent to draw an opponent, the patience to delay the pass until commitment is real, and the timing of a lead pass into a moving partner.
- **Why not skip:** students who never work a 2v1 either pass instantly to avoid responsibility or refuse to pass at all; both habits survive into full games, where the free player is repeatedly ignored.
