# 1. Lesson identity
**Name/code:** Turn and Protect the Ball; FB-03-EN
**Sport/subtopic:** Football; turn, body position, escape pressure
**Age/experience:** 10–12; beginner–developing
**Duration/students:** 45 minutes; 20–30
**Group:** Girls, boys, or mixed; adapt by ability
**Equipment:** 10–15 balls, 30 cones, bibs
**Space/complexity:** flat field or gym; intermediate
# 2. Overall purpose
Develop control that allows a change of direction and movement away from pressure through body position, without contact or relying only on speed.
# 3. Observable learning objectives
- Stop and turn within two touches in 6/8.
- Place the body between the shadow defender and the ball in 5/7.
- Exit the turn with the ball within one step.
- Scan and select an open gate.
- Complete a controlled 1v1 without contact.
# 4. Required prior knowledge
Controlled dribble, sole stop, and basic pass. Students needing support work unopposed at walking pace.
# 5. Position in the learning pathway
- **Previous:** Football 02—pass and support.
- **Current:** It adds the answer to pressure, teaching students to stop, turn, and place the body between an opponent and the ball before leaving through open space; it withholds shooting and any finish on goal — every escape ends at a gate, not a target — which Football 04 adds.
- **Next:** Football 04—drive through a gate and finish accurately.
# 6. Lesson contribution
- **Motor:** stop, turn, accelerate.
- **Social:** respect boundaries.
- **Cognitive:** read pressure and choose side.
- **Tactical:** use the body and exit pressure.
- **Emotional:** composure and confidence.
# 7. Equipment and space
Five 6×8 metre areas with coloured gates; a ball per pair. **Setup:** 7 minutes; **pack-down:** 4. One active pair and an outside observer per area.
# 8. Safety
- No tackling, pushing, or pulling.
- Shadow defender 50%, then 65%.
- The ball stays down; no elbow use.
- One pair active; stop at the boundary.
- Retrieve after scanning; check surface and laces.
# 9. Lesson sequence
## Opening
3 minutes. “Protect by position, not contact: body between pressure and ball, then leave through open space.” Purpose: three principles.
## Warm-up
7 minutes. Dribble into a square, sole-stop, turn, and exit a coloured gate. 3 minutes walking, 2 with both feet, 2 with a later cue. **Variations:** soft ball, wide gate.
## Main activity A
10 minutes. Dribble to the central cone, sole-stop, turn 90–180 degrees, exit an open gate. Six each side. **Cues:** bent knees, close ball, scan before exit. **Errors:** loose ball, tall turn, many steps, eyes down.
## Main activity B
10 minutes. The attacker receives with the defender one-and-a-half arms away. The defender closes one side without contact; the attacker positions the body and exits the other side. Switch every two. **Cues:** side-on shoulder, natural arms, first touch away. **Errors:** leaning, elbow, exit into the defender, defender too close.
## Applied game
10 minutes. Controlled 1v1; the attacker scores for a controlled open-gate crossing, the defender for closing one without contact; both gain a safety point. **Variations:** shadow; 65%; three gates.
## Closure
5 minutes. **Message:** “Turn with the ball close, put your body between the defender and the ball, and let your first touch take you out the open side.” Demonstration, student explains choice, collection.
# 10. Common errors
- **Ball too far:** the defender can reach; use a smaller touch.
- **Body not between:** direct pressure line; rotate shoulder and hip.
- **Elbow use:** unsafe contact; natural close arms.
- **No scan:** closed-gate exit; identify colour first.
- **Tall turn:** loss of balance; bend knees.
- **Early acceleration:** the ball escapes; control, then go.
# 11. Teaching points
- Slow before turn.
- Keep close.
- Body between.
- Natural arms.
- Scan open side.
- First touch away.
- Accelerate after control.
# 12. Adaptations
**Foundation:** no defender, walk, wide gate. **Intermediate:** 50% shadow. **Advanced:** 65%, late cue, three gates. **Large group:** six areas. **Small group:** rotating pairs. **Limited space:** 4×6. **Limited equipment:** one ball per trio. **Different needs:** short route, soft ball, visual/sound cue, fixed partner, partial turn.
# 13. Assessment
Control, body position, gate choice, exit, safety. **Success:** at least four of the five objectives achieved, each by its own stated measure. **Partial:** three objectives, or achieved unopposed only. **Not yet:** contact, loose ball, closed-gate choice. Return to Main A.
# 14. Reflection
**Teacher:** Was defender distance safe and which turn worked? Student: Where was the defender and where did you exit? Improve: speed and gate spacing.
# 15. Continuity
**If achieved:** Football 04—drive and finish. **If not:** repeat Main A; return to Football 01 for control.

# 19. Pedagogical value
- **Why important:** it gives students a legal, safe answer to being closed down; without one, the instinctive responses are to kick the ball away blindly or to hold the opponent off with the arms.
- **Develops:** turning with the ball under close control, side-on body position that shields without fouling, reading which side the pressure is coming from, and the composure to control before accelerating.
- **Why not skip:** pressure is the defining feature of match play, and a student who cannot turn out of it loses possession every time; the alternative habits they invent — panic clearances and elbows — are both ineffective and unsafe.
