# 1. Lesson identity
**Name/code:** 4v4 Small-Sided Transition Game; FB-09-EN
**Sport/subtopic:** Football; width, support, possession transition
**Age/experience:** 13–16; developing
**Duration/students:** 45 minutes; 16–32
**Group:** Girls, boys, or mixed; adapt by ability
**Equipment:** 4–6 balls, 32 cones, bibs, 8–12 small goals
**Space/complexity:** field or gym; advanced
# 2. Overall purpose
Combine passing, movement, advantage, and defensive transition in a small-sided game that gives every student repeated decisions and participation.
# 3. Observable learning objectives
- Create at least two passing options around the ball.
- Switch role within two seconds of a possession change.
- Use width rather than crowding the centre.
- Defend without contact and protect the central gate.
- Each student makes at least three meaningful actions per game.
# 4. Required prior knowledge
Passing, receiving, movement, defence, 2v1, and transition from Lessons 02–08. Students needing support use a neutral player or numerical advantage.
# 5. Position in the learning pathway
- **Previous:** Football 08—transition after loss.
- **Current:** It removes the signals and stoppages, running continuous 4v4 in which width, support, advantage, and transition must all be produced by the students themselves; it withholds only the formal assessment of each individual's pathway, which Football 10 adds as the closing lesson.
- **Next:** Football 10—final applied game and assessment.
# 6. Lesson contribution
- **Motor:** integrated actions.
- **Social:** cooperation and fairness.
- **Cognitive:** changing decisions.
- **Tactical:** width, support, transition.
- **Emotional:** independence and handling results.
# 7. Equipment and space
Three or four 16×22 metre courts with two goals per side. **Setup:** 10 minutes; **pack-down:** 6. Teams of 4–5, three-minute games and rotations.
# 8. Safety
- No tackle, slide, or contact.
- Stable goals and nobody behind them.
- Low ball.
- Stop out of bounds; defence up to 75%.
- Retrieve after scan; rest for fatigue/pain; the teacher stops uncontrolled play.
# 9. Lesson sequence
## Opening
3 minutes. “We measure quality decisions and inclusion, not goals alone.” Purpose: participation and transition rules.
## Warm-up
7 minutes. 4v1 keep-ball; after a turnover, the losing player and the nearest switch role within two seconds. **Variations:** no defender, two-touch, larger area.
## Main activity A
8 minutes. Four retain against two; after four passes, exit a side gate. On loss, attackers recover to central cones. **Cues:** width, open body, response. **Errors:** crowding, long ball, all press.
## Main activity B
10 minutes. Attackers target two goals; defenders protect the centre. Roles switch after loss. Score only when three attackers touched. **Cues:** free player, pass into space, nearest delays and far recovers.
## Applied game
12 minutes. Three-minute games. **Scoring:** one goal; bonus when three different players touch the ball in the attack; bonus when defence restores shape within five seconds. No contact, maximum three touches. **Variations:** neutral player; side goals; no touch limit for foundation.
## Closure
5 minutes. **Message:** “Width with ball; protect centre without it.” Team example, collection, count.
# 10. Common errors
- **All central:** mark side channels.
- **Long dribble:** three-touch limit.
- **Excluded player:** award a bonus when three different players have touched the ball during the attack.
- **All press after loss:** near/far roles.
- **Contact defence:** distance and delay.
- **Power kicks:** small goal and low strike.
# 11. Teaching points
- Width in attack.
- Two options.
- Open body.
- Quick role switch.
- Nearest delays.
- Far recovers centrally.
- Include all.
- Decision before speed.
# 12. Adaptations
**Foundation:** 4v3/neutral, no touch limit. **Intermediate:** 4v4, three touches. **Advanced:** two-touch and five seconds. **Large group:** four courts. **Small group:** 3v3. **Limited space:** 12×16. **Limited equipment:** cone goals. **Different needs:** fixed support zone, soft ball, extra time, partner, neutral role.
# 13. Assessment
Width, support, transition, participation, safety. **Success:** 4/5 in two games. **Partial:** three, or with numerical advantage. **Not yet:** crowding, exclusion, contact. Return to 4v3.
# 14. Reflection
**Teacher:** Did all participate and did the rules help? Student: What did you do at possession change? Improve: one rule to change next round.
# 15. Continuity
**If achieved:** Football 10—final assessment. **If not:** return to the limiting component—05 movement, 06 defence, 07 advantage, or 08 transition.

# 19. Pedagogical value
- **Why important:** small-sided play multiplies the number of touches and decisions each student gets, so it reveals whether the pathway has transferred — something a full-sided match, where most of the class barely touches the ball, cannot show.
- **Develops:** the integration of passing, movement, overload, and transition into continuous play, shared responsibility for including every team-mate, and the composure to keep deciding while tired.
- **Why not skip:** skills rehearsed only in isolated drills may never appear in a game; without this step the unit ends without evidence, and the students who most need coaching are exactly the ones a large match would hide.
