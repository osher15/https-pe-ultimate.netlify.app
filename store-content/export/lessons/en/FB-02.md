# 1. Lesson identity
**Name/code:** Pass, Receive, and Move to Support; FB-02-EN
**Sport/subtopic:** Football; inside-foot pass, first touch, support
**Age/experience:** 9–11; beginner–developing
**Duration/students:** 45 minutes; 20–30
**Group:** Girls, boys, or mixed; adapt by ability
**Equipment:** 10–15 balls, 28 cones, bibs
**Space/complexity:** flat field or gym; intermediate
# 2. Overall purpose
Develop accurate passing, a safe first touch, and immediate movement to a new support point as foundations of team play.
# 3. Observable learning objectives
- Pass with the inside foot into a one-metre area around the partner in 6/8.
- Keep the first touch within one step.
- Scan before passing and form a support angle afterward.
- Maintain 3–5 metre triangle spacing.
- Complete five sequences without contact or leaving the area.
# 4. Required prior knowledge
Basic control and dribbling into space from Football 01. Students needing support use a shorter distance and a softer ball.
# 5. Position in the learning pathway
- **Previous:** Football 01—control and dribble.
- **Current:** It adds the second player, turning the solo control of Football 01 into an inside-foot pass, a first touch that keeps the ball within one step, and a move to a new support angle straight after release; it withholds turning away from pressure and any defender permitted to tackle, so every pass is made in open space — which Football 03 changes.
- **Next:** Football 03—change direction and protect the ball.
# 6. Lesson contribution
- **Motor:** accuracy and foot–eye coordination.
- **Social:** cooperation.
- **Cognitive:** scanning and timing.
- **Tactical:** support angle and width.
- **Emotional:** trust and recovery after loss.
# 7. Equipment and space
One ball per pair/trio; 5-metre triangles and 7×7 squares. **Setup:** 7 minutes; **pack-down:** 4. Keep 1.5-metre walkways; no queues.
# 8. Safety
- Ball on the ground.
- No tackling during teaching.
- Pass to a watching partner; remain in the area.
- Retrieve a loose ball after scanning.
- No power kicks; two-arm spacing.
- Check surface and laces.
# 9. Lesson sequence
## Opening
3 minutes. “A pass continues through the receiver’s touch and the passer’s movement.” Purpose: three-part sequence.
## Warm-up
7 minutes. Pairs roll, sole-stop, and move to a new cone. At minute 3 use an inside-foot pass; at minute 5 add a name call. **Variations:** shorter distance, larger ball.
## Main activity A
10 minutes. Pairs pass through a one-metre gate; eight passes then change angle. **Cues:** support foot beside ball, firm ankle, inside foot to target, soft touch. **Errors:** toe kick, distant support foot, excess force, static reception.
## Main activity B
10 minutes. Trio triangle; after passing, fill the empty corner. Four circuits each direction. **Cues:** scan, wide angle, first touch to space, move after control. **Errors:** all approach the ball, standing still, pass behind, loose touch.
## Applied game
10 minutes. 3v1 in a square; shadow defender up to 50%, no tackle. Four passes unlock an end-gate pass. Score one for four passes and a bonus if all receive. **Variations:** no defender; 60%; two gates.
## Closure
5 minutes. **Message:** “Your job is not finished when the ball leaves your foot — move at once to a place where your partner can find you again.” Demonstration, partner feedback, organised collection.
# 10. Common errors
- **Toe kick:** poor accuracy; open the inside foot.
- **Support foot far:** the body falls; mark a footprint beside the ball.
- **Pass too hard:** the touch escapes; shorten the distance.
- **No movement:** the triangle closes; use a bright empty cone.
- **Touch under the body:** no continuation; direct the touch to the open side.
- **Eyes down:** the partner is unready; use name call and scan.
# 11. Teaching points
- Support foot beside ball.
- Firm ankle.
- Ground pass.
- Soft touch.
- Scan first.
- Pass-and-move.
- Wide triangle.
- Brief communication.
# 12. Adaptations
**Foundation:** soft ball, 2–3 metres, wide gate. **Intermediate:** 4–5 metres and movement. **Advanced:** two-touch and 60% defender. **Large group:** eight triangles. **Small group:** 3v1. **Limited space:** 4×4 walking. **Limited equipment:** one ball per four plus observer. **Different needs:** large target, slow pace, fixed partner, visual/sound cue, individual plan.
# 13. Assessment
Accuracy, touch, scan, movement, spacing. **Success:** at least four of the five objectives achieved, each by its own stated measure. **Partial:** three objectives, or achieved unopposed only. **Not yet:** repeated loose touch, no movement, collision risk. Return to the pair gate.
# 14. Reflection
**Teacher:** Were distance and touches suitable? Student: Where did your first touch go and why move after the pass? Improve: gates and distances.
# 15. Continuity
**If achieved:** Football 03—turn and protect. **If not:** repeat Main A; return to Football 01 for control.

# 19. Pedagogical value
- **Why important:** it converts individual control into cooperation: the pass is the moment football stops being one student with a ball and becomes a team solving a problem together.
- **Develops:** passing accuracy with the inside of the foot, a first touch that sets up the next action, the scan that precedes release, and the timing of arriving at a support angle before the ball needs to move again.
- **Why not skip:** without it, play is dominated by whoever dribbles longest, the rest of the class crowds the ball, and later small-sided lessons have no passing network to build on.
