# 1. Lesson identity
**Name/code:** 3v3 Small-Sided Decision Game; BB-10-EN
**Sport/subtopic:** Basketball; integrated support, drive, pass, and defence
**Age/experience:** 12–15; developing
**Duration/students:** 45 minutes; 18–30
**Group:** Girls, boys, or mixed; adapt by ability
**Equipment:** 4–6 balls, 24 cones, bibs, 2–4 baskets/gates
**Space/complexity:** half courts or 12×15-metre areas; advanced
# 2. Overall purpose
Enable students to combine learned skills and tactics into independent choices within safe, equitable small-sided play.
# 3. Observable learning objectives
- Team creates two support points around the ball in most attacks.
- Ball carrier scans and chooses pass, dribble, or finish within three seconds.
- Passer moves to a new lane in 6/8 opportunities.
- Defence controls a route without contact.
- Every student performs reception, support, and defensive roles.
# 4. Required prior knowledge
Dribble, pass/catch, off-ball movement, close finish, no-contact defence, and 2v1. Missing components receive a support rule or adapted role.
# 5. Position in the learning pathway
- **Previous:** Basketball 09—use 2v1.
- **Current:** It removes every remaining scaffold, asking students to combine support, dribble, pass, finish, and no-contact defence into independent choices in continuous 3v3; nothing further is withheld inside this pathway — what it leaves to a later unit is full-sided play, set offences, and contested rebounding.
- **Next:** Football 02 or an advanced basketball unit after teacher assessment.
# 6. Lesson contribution
- **Motor:** integrated skills.
- **Social:** cooperation and fairness.
- **Cognitive:** changing choices.
- **Tactical:** width, support, advantage, defensive recovery.
- **Emotional:** independence and handling outcomes.
# 7. Equipment and space
Create 3–5 small courts with basket or cone gate; ball and bibs per game. **Setup:** 9 minutes; **pack-down:** 5. Fixed teams of 3–4; timed games and rotations, no long queues.
# 8. Safety
- No contact, physical screens, or taking from hands.
- Stop at boundaries; clear the basket area before restart.
- Retrieve loose ball after scanning; no face pass.
- Defence maximum 75%.
- Substitute for fatigue, pain, or dizziness; the teacher stops uncontrolled play.
# 9. Lesson sequence
## Opening
3 minutes. “Today success is not about the most baskets; it is creating options and choosing well.” Purpose: criteria for fair, intelligent play.
## Warm-up
7 minutes. Moving triangle passes; fill the empty point after passing. Add a central shadow defender during minutes 4–7. **Variations:** walk, soft ball, larger area.
## Main activity A
8 minutes. The team crosses an area using at least one pass, dribble, and finish in any order. An observer records width and movement. **Errors:** fixed pattern, everyone toward the ball, one action only.
## Main activity B
10 minutes. Three attackers versus two defenders up to 70%; change roles after turnover. Point only when two support points existed. **Cues:** scan, use the free player, defend between ball and target. **Errors:** crowding, over-dribble, contact, excluded player.
## Applied game
12 minutes. Three-minute games. **Rules:** three-second possession, no contact, move after passing. **Scoring:** one for finish/gate; bonus when all three touch; bonus for a no-contact defensive stop. **Variations:** no dribble; two bounces; end zone; neutral player.
## Closure
5 minutes. **Message:** “Good play is a sequence of team-helping decisions.” Teams name one successful choice, then collect and count equipment.
# 10. Common errors
- **Everyone crowds the ball:** use three floor points.
- **Long dribble:** limit to two bounces.
- **Standing after pass:** apply the pass-and-move rule.
- **Player excluded:** bonus when all three participate.
- **Contact defence:** restore one-and-a-half-arm spacing and no taking from hands.
- **Speed without scan:** require one look before action.
# 11. Teaching points
- Width and support.
- Scan before action.
- Three seconds.
- Pass and move.
- Use the free player.
- Defend by position.
- Include everyone.
- Decision before result.
# 12. Adaptations
**Foundation:** 3v2 or neutral player. **Intermediate:** 3v3, two bounces. **Advanced:** three-second 3v3. **Large group:** five courts. **Small group:** 2v2 plus neutral. **Limited space:** 8×10, no basket. **Limited equipment:** gates and one ball per game. **Different needs:** fixed support zone, soft ball, no dribble, extra time, peer partner, individual plan.
# 13. Assessment
Width, scan, movement, choice, safe defence. **Success:** 4/5 in two consecutive games. **Partial:** three criteria, or success only with advantage. **Not yet:** crowding, exclusion, repeated contact. Return to 3v2 or unopposed.
# 14. Reflection
**Teacher:** Who participated, did the rules promote learning, was the area suitable? Student: Which choice helped and what did you do off-ball? Improve: select one rule only next round.
# 15. Continuity
**If achieved:** advanced game unit or Football 02. **If not:** return to the limiting lesson—05 movement, 06 choice, 08 defence, or 09 advantage.

# 19. Pedagogical value
- **Why important:** it is the only point in the pathway where a teacher can see whether taught skills have become independent game behaviour, because nothing here is cued, signalled, or rehearsed in advance.
- **Develops:** the integration of support, dribble, pass, finish, and defence into continuous play, shared responsibility for including every team member, and the composure to keep choosing well after a mistake.
- **Why not skip:** skills demonstrated only in drills may never transfer; without an applied game the unit ends with no evidence of learning, and students leave able to perform basketball actions without being able to play.
