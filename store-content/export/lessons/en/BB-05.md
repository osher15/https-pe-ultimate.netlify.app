# 1. Lesson identity
**Full name:** Off-Ball Movement and Passing Lanes
**Lesson code:** BB-05-EN
**Sport:** Basketball
**Subtopic:** getting open, supporting the ball, and communication
**Age:** 10–12
**Experience:** beginner–developing
**Duration:** 45 minutes
**Students:** 20–30
**Group:** Girls, boys, or mixed; adapt by ability and need
**Equipment:** 8–12 balls, 24 cones, 10–15 bibs
**Space:** full or half basketball court
**Complexity:** intermediate
# 2. Overall purpose
Develop the habit of creating a new passing option after the ball moves, as a foundation for continuous team play and decision-making.
# 3. Observable learning objectives
- By the end, students will:
- Move to an open point within three seconds after passing in six of eight sequences.
- Show a clear two-hand target and make eye contact before reception.
- Create an angle that is not directly behind a shadow defender.
- Maintain approximately 2–4 metres of team spacing during most play.
- Use one brief verbal or visual communication cue appropriately.
# 4. Required prior knowledge
Students should perform a basic chest pass and controlled catch, understand court boundaries, and move safely in shared space. Students not yet ready use the no-defender version.
# 5. Position in the learning pathway
- **Previous lesson:** Basketball 04—close-range shooting and safe rebounding; students learned to set their body and act after receiving.
- **Current lesson:** It adds the off-ball half of team play: Basketball 04 ended at the shot, and here students replace standing after a pass with an immediate move into a new lane, plus the hand target and brief call that make them receivable. It withholds what to do once the ball arrives — reception here always leads to another pass, never a dribble or a shot — which Basketball 06 opens up.
- **Next lesson:** Basketball 06—receive while moving and choose to pass or dribble according to space.
# 6. Lesson contribution
- **Motor:** change of direction, stopping, short acceleration, receiving on the move.
- **Social:** communication, role rotation, shared-space responsibility, respectful feedback.
- **Cognitive:** scanning, locating space, rapid choice.
- **Tactical:** passing angles, width, support.
- **Emotional:** confidence to offer for the ball and recover from mistakes.
# 7. Equipment and space
- **Detail:** one ball per pair/trio; four cones per square; one defender bib per group.
- **Setup:** 6 minutes before class.
- **Pack-down:** 4 minutes after class with assigned equipment teams.
- **Recommended organisation:** five or six 6×6-metre squares with 1.5-metre walkways; 4–5 students per square. On half court, use 5×5-metre squares and rotate groups.
# 8. Safety
- No body contact, screening, or taking the ball from hands.
- Shadow defenders stay at the stated speed and avoid late cuts into a runner.
- Retrieve a loose ball only after stopping and checking the walkway.
- Never pass towards a face; pass after eye contact or a visible hand target.
- Stay inside the assigned square and keep walkways clear.
- Check tied laces, dry surface, and stable flat cones.
- Stop and report pain, dizziness, or unusual difficulty.
# 9. Lesson sequence
## Opening
**Time:** 3 minutes  
**Teacher says:** “A pass is only part of the action. Today you help again by creating a new option immediately.”  
**Purpose:** introduce “pass–scan–move” and safety boundaries.
## Warm-up
**Time:** 7 minutes  
**Purpose:** warm the body, change direction, and identify free space without overload.  
**Structure:** groups of four in a square; three cones occupied, one empty.  
**Instructions:** a student indicates the free cone; another moves there, balances, and shows hands. After two minutes, add a short pass followed by movement.  
**Transitions:** add the ball at minute 3; progress from walking to light jogging at minute 5.  
**Variations:** use a colour cue; shorten the route for a student with movement needs.
## Main activity A
**Time:** 10 minutes  
**Purpose:** build movement after passing as a habit.  
**Process:** three students form a triangle. A passes to B and immediately fills the empty cone; B turns to the ball and passes to C. Reverse after four circuits.  
**Organisation:** one ball and three cones per trio, 3–4 metres apart.  
**Teaching points:** move at an angle, show hands, wait for controlled reception, keep a wide triangle.  
**Common errors:** standing after passing, running behind the receiver, crowding, passing before readiness.
## Main activity B
**Time:** 10 minutes  
**Purpose:** read how a defender changes the support angle.  
**Process:** three attackers and one shadow defender. The defender works at 50% and blocks one lane without stealing. Attackers complete six passes; each passer moves to a point where the ball can be seen.  
**Organisation:** square, ball, and bib; rotate defender every 90 seconds.  
**Teaching points:** scan first, change angle rather than only distance, communicate briefly, stop where a pass is possible.  
**Common errors:** everyone approaches the ball, straight-line movement, defender too fast, attacker hidden.
## Applied game
**Time:** 10 minutes  
**Purpose:** apply support in small-sided play.  
**Rules:** 3v3 or 4v4, no dribble, no contact, three-second possession, and the passer must leave the spot.  
**Scoring:** one point for six passes; bonus point when three different players receive after moving to open space.  
**Variations:** support—one fewer defender; standard—equal numbers; challenge—two-second possession or a moving reception in an end zone.
## Closure
**Time:** 5 minutes  
**Central message:** “A good pass finishes when I create the next option.”  
**Learning review:** two students demonstrate pass–scan–move; peers identify the new angle.  
**Feedback:** each student names one action that made them available.  
**Orderly exit:** assigned groups collect balls, cones, and bibs; teacher counts and dismisses.
# 10. Common errors
- **Standing after a pass:** the same spot remains occupied; use the cue “pass and move” plus an empty cone.
- **Running straight toward the ball:** spacing collapses; direct a diagonal route to a side cone.
- **Hiding behind a defender:** the passer cannot see the target hands; sidestep until a sight line appears.
- **Crowding:** two attackers are within two arm lengths; pause and rebuild a wide triangle.
- **No eye contact:** the receiver is surprised; require name or hand target before release.
- **Leaving too early:** the student moves before the catch is controlled; add a brief hold until reception.
# 11. Teaching points
- Pass–scan–move.
- Angle before distance.
- Show hands.
- Keep 2–4 metres.
- Stop balanced and face the ball.
- Communicate briefly.
- Correct one point at a time.
# 12. Adaptations
- **Foundation:** no defender, walking, larger square, soft ball.
- **Intermediate:** shadow defender at 50%, three-second hold.
- **Advanced:** defender up to 75%, two-second hold, moving reception.
- **Large group:** six small squares and rotating observer; no queues.
- **Small group:** 3v2 or triangle with one defender.
- **Limited space:** 4×4 squares, walking, short passes.
- **Limited equipment:** one ball per four; parallel no-ball movement task.
- **Different needs:** strong visual marks, one instruction at a time, fixed partner, closer support points, or approved individual plan.
# 13. Assessment
- **Checks:** response time, angle, hand target, spacing, communication.
- **Success:** four of five criteria in six of eight sequences.
- **Partial success:** three criteria or reliable performance only without a defender.
- **Not yet successful:** remains stationary, creates collision risk, or cannot offer a lane after prompting.
- **Response:** return to the unopposed triangle or two-cone walking rehearsal.
# 14. Reflection
- **Teacher:** Was activity time high? Were squares suitable? Who needed a cue? Was defender intensity safe?
- **Students:** How did you choose where to move? What helped the passer see you? When did movement close space?
- **Next improvement:** adjust area size and identify who is ready for pass-or-dribble decisions.
# 15. Continuity
- **If achieved:** Basketball 06—receive on the move and decide pass or dribble.
- **If not achieved:** repeat “pass and fill” without a defender; return to Basketball 03 if pass/catch accuracy is the barrier.
