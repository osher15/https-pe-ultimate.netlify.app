# 1. Lesson identity
**Name/code:** Off-Ball Movement to Receive; FB-05-EN
**Sport/subtopic:** Football; check away, change direction, receive in space
**Age/experience:** 11–14; developing
**Duration/students:** 45 minutes; 20–30
**Group:** Girls, boys, or mixed; adapt by ability
**Equipment:** 8–12 balls, 28 cones, bibs
**Space/complexity:** field or gym; intermediate
# 2. Overall purpose
Develop timed movement into open space before reception, creating a passing lane and preparing the next attacking action.
# 3. Observable learning objectives
- Check away, then change direction to receive in 6/8.
- Reach space as the passer becomes ready.
- Use a body position that sees ball and target.
- First touch into safe space in 5/7.
- Maintain spacing without contact against a shadow.
# 4. Required prior knowledge
Pass/receive from Football 02, turn from Football 03, finish from Football 04. Students needing support work unopposed at walking pace.
# 5. Position in the learning pathway
- **Previous:** Football 04—drive and finish.
- **Current:** It adds the movement that happens before the ball arrives — checking away, changing direction, and timing arrival to the passer's head-up moment — so the receiver creates the lane instead of waiting in it; it withholds the defensive half of that picture, since the shadow here never competes for the ball, which Football 06 supplies.
- **Next:** Football 06—no-contact defence and closing passing lanes.
# 6. Lesson contribution
- **Motor:** change pace/direction.
- **Social:** coordination.
- **Cognitive:** timing and scan.
- **Tactical:** passing lane and width.
- **Emotional:** initiative and confidence.
# 7. Equipment and space
Five or six 6×10 metre rectangles with a passer point and two receiving gates. **Setup:** 7 minutes; **pack-down:** 4. Groups of 4–5; outside return.
# 8. Safety
- No contact or screening.
- Shadow at 50%.
- One active pair; ground pass.
- Stay in the area; stop at the boundary.
- Retrieve after scanning; check surface and laces.
# 9. Lesson sequence
## Opening
3 minutes. “To receive, sometimes move away first, then arrive as space opens.” Purpose: check–change–receive.
## Warm-up
7 minutes. One partner points at a gate; the receiver first moves opposite, changes, then receives a soft pass. 3 minutes without a ball, 4 with a ball. **Variations:** walk, colour cue, closer gate.
## Main activity A
10 minutes. The receiver moves two steps away from the receiving cone, changes, and enters an open gate. Six each side. **Cues:** watch the passer, controlled sharp change, half-open body, first touch forward. **Errors:** early run, straight line, back to target, stop after reception.
## Main activity B
10 minutes. The shadow defender stands between passer and receiver and closes one line at 50%. The receiver changes angle to the other gate. Switch every two. **Cues:** become visible, call briefly, time with the passer’s head-up moment. **Errors:** approach the passer, cross paths, defender too fast, late pass.
## Applied game
10 minutes. 3v1 in a square with two side gates. Score for receiving through a gate after a direction change; bonus when the next touch prepares a finish into a small goal. **Variations:** no defender; shadow; 65%.
## Closure
5 minutes. **Message:** “Move when the passer is ready, not after the ball has left.” Demonstration, one feedback sentence, collection.
# 10. Common errors
- **Early run:** space closes; move as the passer looks up.
- **Straight route:** the defender stays in line; check away and angle.
- **Back to target:** no next action; half-open body.
- **Too close:** space shrinks; mark the receiving gate.
- **No communication:** the passer misses the cue; use a name or a raised hand.
- **Dead first touch:** the attack stops; touch toward the next target.
# 11. Teaching points
- Check away.
- Time with passer.
- Show a lane.
- Half-open body.
- Brief call.
- Touch into space.
- Continue after reception.
# 12. Adaptations
**Foundation:** no defender, walk, wide gate. **Intermediate:** 50% shadow. **Advanced:** 65%, two gates, two-touch. **Large group:** six areas. **Small group:** 3v1. **Limited space:** 4×8. **Limited equipment:** one ball per four. **Different needs:** short route, bright mark, sound/visual cue, fixed partner, fixed receiving role.
# 13. Assessment
Timing, direction change, lane, body, touch. **Success:** at least four of the five objectives achieved, each by its own stated measure. **Partial:** three objectives, or achieved unopposed. **Not yet:** early movement, hidden position, collision risk. Return to Main A.
# 14. Reflection
**Teacher:** Was the cue clear and the shadow fair? Student: When did you move and what did you see? Improve: distance, timing, angle.
# 15. Continuity
**If achieved:** Football 06—close passing lane. **If not:** repeat Main A; return to Football 02 for passing.

# 19. Pedagogical value
- **Why important:** it teaches that attacking is created without the ball: most of a match is spent off it, and the quality of those movements decides whether a pass is ever available.
- **Develops:** timing a run to the passer's readiness rather than to the ball, changing direction sharply to break a marking line, half-open body orientation that sees ball and target together, and the initiative to act unprompted.
- **Why not skip:** without it, players in possession hold the ball waiting for movement that never comes, the attack stalls, and the class concludes that passing does not work — when the real gap is that nobody moved.
