## Setup and goal
Ages 8–15; 4–6 participants. Each has a 2×2 m square with a metre gap from others. Choose from four actions: two marching steps; a clap in front; comfortable elbow bend and extension; right hand to left shoulder then left hand to right shoulder. Goal: remember a shared chain of up to 6 actions.
## Procedure, rules and ending
First chooses an action and everyone performs it. Next repeats and adds one; everyone performs the new chain. Reusing an action is allowed. Complete 6 actions: team point, short rest and new chain with another starter. A request for help pauses the chain: repeat at the same length with demonstration, without a point. After an assisted six-action chain, start a new chain with a different first participant. For a shorter chain, resume adding one action per turn; if it remains difficult, remove one action and retry. Nobody is eliminated; everyone adds in turn, even after an error.
## Safety and adaptations
No jumps, fast turns or leaving squares. Use comfortable arm range, without stretching. Seated players may replace marching with alternating heel lifts, agreed as equivalent beforehand. Easier: only 3 actions with reminder cards, scored separately. Harder: no demonstration before recall, not faster movement.
## Timing and assessment
8 minutes: 2 explanation and trial, 2 play, 1 rest, 2 play, 1 reflection. Record longest unprompted group chain and completed chains of 6. Never publicly record who forgot.
