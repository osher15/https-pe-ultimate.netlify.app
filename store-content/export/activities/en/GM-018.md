## Setup and goal
Ages 11–17; 6 players in two threes, soft ball and 8 cones. Field 16×12 m with clear 2 m margins. On each end line, two 2 m goals separated by 4 m, with 2 m to each corner. Each team defends two and attacks the opposite two. Goal: recognise open scoring space.
## Procedure, rules and ending
Walking and ground passes only. Score when the whole ball crosses an opposing gate. No goalkeeper; defenders may intercept passes but stay at least a metre from the dribbler and never tackle feet. After scoring, opponents restart centrally with defenders 3 m away. Side or end-line exit not scoring: boundary pass to the team that did not touch last. Defenders stay 3 m away at restarts; a direct goal from a restart earns no point, and the opposing team restarts from that end line. Running, contact or lofted ball: stop and give opponents a pass at that spot. If last touch is unclear, teacher returns possession to the team holding it before the exit.
## Safety and adaptations
No sliding, hard kicks or body blocking. Give the ball holder room to scan; teacher stops crowding. Easier: introductory unopposed play without competitive scoring. Accessible alternative: same goal choice using hand rolls and suitable mobility, without mixing kicks and wheelchairs in one round. Harder: all three participate in passing before a shot.
## Timing and assessment
10 minutes: 2 explanation and trial, 3 play, 1 rest and change ends, 3 play, 1 feedback. Draw starting team; other starts after rest. Scores accumulate; ties stand. Observe goal selection and involvement.
