## Identity and setup
Ages 11–16. Trios in a 4×4 m square; one soft ball, two standing markers, paper and pencil per trio. Passer and receiver about 3 m apart; observer to the side. For 30: 10 balls, 20 markers and 10 sheets.
## Observable goal
Give factual feedback on a two-handed pass and apply one change next attempt; every pupil performs, receives and observes.
## Procedure and timing
12 minutes: 2 feedback demonstration, 9 in three 3-minute rounds, 1 review. Each round: 1 minute for five baseline passes, 1 for description and a change rehearsal, 1 for five repeat passes and recording. Observer checks one agreed goal: ball reaches arm's reach without a large step. Say 'I saw…' and suggest 'Try…'. Passer chooses one change, such as reducing force. Rotate roles each round so all fill all three. An increased number of successful passes is not required to meet the feedback goal.
## Coaching and error correction
Describe actions, not character: 'Three passes went above shoulder height', not 'You're bad'. If several tips are offered, choose one. Receiver keeps the same position for a fair comparison.
## Safety
Soft balls and moderate passes only; never aim at faces. No filming or publishing pupil results. Observer stays outside the passing line. Stop hurtful comments and rephrase.
## Adaptations and assessment
Easier: 2 m distance and feedback sentence starters. Harder: performer explains the chosen adjustment. Access: roll to a stably seated partner or adapt distance, keeping the same conditions before and after. Assess factual description, one change and retesting; counts are personal, not class rankings.
