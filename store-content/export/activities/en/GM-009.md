## Setup and goal
Ages 9–16; 6 participants in two teams. Mark a 3×3 grid with metre-wide cells; provide 3 beanbags per team with different symbols. Waiting line 2 m from the grid; total area 5×8 m. Goal: choose moves to complete a line of 3.
## Procedure, rules and ending
Teams alternate; a different member walks out each turn, places a bag in an empty cell and returns before the next person leaves. Once all 6 bags are placed, move one of your team's bags to an empty cell per turn. A row, column or diagonal of 3 ends the game. Undo an illegal move and choose again without penalty. After 2 minutes without a winner: draw and reset. Alternate the starting team.
## Safety and adaptations
Walking only, one person at the grid, no jumping over bags. Never move an opponent's bag. Teams may discuss before leaving. For access, use a raised grid; players select and place or direct an assistant of their choice. Easier: consider two possible moves with the teacher. Harder: explain how a move blocks or creates a threat.
## Timing and assessment
8 minutes: 1 explanation, 6 play and resets, 1 discussion. Restart early-finished games until the play window ends. Record turn sharing and explanation of one move; winning is not an individual grade.
