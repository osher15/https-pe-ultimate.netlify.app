## Setup and goal
Ages 12–16; trio, secured adapted basket and soft ball, clear 5×5 m space. Start close without running. Goal: coordinate step, small takeoff and one-hand release.
## Procedure and timing
7 minutes: 2 demonstration, 4 practice, 1 feedback. Right side: hold ball, step left, small takeoff from left and comfortable right-knee lift; gently release with right hand under ball. Reverse for left side. Shooter, collector to side, observer behind; three attempts then rotate. Collect only after ball lands.
## Coaching and safety
No rim hanging, contested rebounds or maximum jump. Controlled landing in clear space; stop for lost coordination or pain. Preparation for moving shots, not a full dribble approach.
## Adaptations and assessment
Easier: step and release without jump. Harder: add slow preparation step only after control. Seated one-hand release to low target is a different alternative. Assess leg–hand sequence and landing in three attempts, not only baskets.
