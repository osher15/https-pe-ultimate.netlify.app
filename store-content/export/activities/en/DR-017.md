## Setup and goal
Ages 9–14; pairs, one bouncy foam ball and a sound, smooth wall without windows or projections. Station 3 m wide and 4 m deep; line 2 m from wall. Goal: move into the rebound path and catch in four of five attempts.
## Procedure and timing
6 minutes: 1 demonstration, 4 practice and swaps, 1 feedback and collection. Toss underarm with both hands at a comfortable wall height below the head, allow one floor bounce and catch with both hands. Swap after five attempts. Observer stands behind and to the side, outside the ball path.
## Coaching and safety
Hands ready in front; short movement into the ball path; bend elbows on catching. Rebound too fast: reduce force, rather than moving closer to the wall. No diving, chasing into another station or throwing during retrieval. For an escaped ball, stop and retrieve at a walk when clear.
## Adaptations and assessment
Easier: two floor bounces. Harder: catch before the floor bounce, only at controlled speed. Stable seated practice can use an accessible ball and rebound height; do not reach beyond the support base. Record successes, actual attempts and version; do not compare scores across versions.
