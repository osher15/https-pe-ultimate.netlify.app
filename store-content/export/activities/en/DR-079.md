## Setup and goal
Ages 13–17; pair, mat and 2×3 m space. Goal: hold a short side-support position while breathing normally. Comfortable forearm support required.
## Procedure
Lie on the side with knees bent and elbow under the shoulder and forearm resting on the mat. Press through forearm and knees, lifting hips slightly. Hold up to 5 seconds, lower and rest 20 seconds. Take 2 attempts per side, then swap; reaching the maximum time is not required.
## Safety and adaptations
Do not lean on the head or hold the breath. Stop for shoulder discomfort. Easier: keep hips on the mat and practise setup. Harder: steadier at the same duration, without straightening legs. Standing alternative: sideways forearm support against a wall, assessed separately.
## Timing and assessment
Record comfortable duration and steadiness across the 2 attempts, without a maximal endurance test. Observer never pushes, lifts or holds the performer.
Suggested duration: 6 minutes — 1 for demonstration, 4 for turns, retrieval and rests, 1 for feedback. Start after appropriate physical preparation; stop for pain or dizziness.
