## Setup and goal
Ages 11–16; pair, soft ball and 3×7 m channel. Start 4 m apart. Goal: controlled two-handed pass from above forehead to ready hands without arching back.
## Procedure and timing
6 minutes: 1 demonstration, 4 practice and pauses, 1 feedback and collection. Hold ball slightly above forehead, elbows comfortably bent. When receiver is ready, extend arms forward and release on a comfortable path towards hands shown in front of chest. Optional short step, no jump. Catch and return same way. Four passes each, then rest.
## Coaching and safety
Do not lower ball far behind head or throw hard towards face. If pass requires backward lean, shorten distance or lighten ball. Shoulder pain: stop. Dropped ball: both stop before retrieval; no diving saves.
## Adaptations and assessment
Easier: 3 m and lighter ball. Harder: ready hands slightly to one side within comfortable reach, stationary during release. Stable seated version may adapt height and distance. Observe three passes for position above forehead, two-handed release and balance; chest pass remains a different alternative.
