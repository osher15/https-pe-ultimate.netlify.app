## Identity and setup
Ages 7–12. Four pupils per 3×7 m lane: two pairs, four soft balls and 12 flat markers per lane. Near zone: 1 m wide and 1 m deep, from 2 to 3 m beyond the roll line. Far zone: 2 m wide and 1 m deep, from 4 to 5 m. Keep the final 2 m clear for ball run-out.
## Observable goal
Control a roll to stop in a declared target zone, wait for the collection signal and score fairly.
## Procedure and timing
8 minutes: 2 explanation and rehearsal, 5 play, 1 review. Before rolling, declare 'near' or 'far'. A ball stopping in the chosen zone scores 1 or 2 points respectively; a boundary counts as inside. Four rolls, one per pupil, then collect together on signal. Alternate which pair starts each round. Out-of-lane balls or balls in an undeclared zone score 0; play continues. If a ball hits an earlier ball, the earlier score stands and the new ball scores by its stopping position. Finish the final round on signal; a tie remains a tie.
## Coaching and error correction
Low release and hand toward target. A bouncing ball calls for a comfortable bend and release near the floor. An overlong roll calls for a smaller swing. Give feedback on direction and force, not strength.
## Safety
One-way lanes; everyone behind the roll line. No retrieval during rolls and no aerial throws. Separate lanes and keep the run-out clear. Stop the affected lane before retrieving a stray ball.
## Adaptations and assessment
Easier: larger near zone. Harder: narrower far zone, not stronger rolls. Stable seated rolling or an adapted ramp is possible. Pairs aim to improve their previous round; no elimination. Teacher records target choice, force adjustment and safe waiting separately from the score.
