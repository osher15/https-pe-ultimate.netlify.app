## Setup and goal
Ages 8–13; no equipment, 2×2 m per pupil. Goal: opposite arm to rising leg and forward–back arms over ten comfortable consecutive steps. Coordination practice, not speed training.
## Procedure
5 minutes: 1 demonstration, 3 practice, 1 checking. Lift knee slightly and advance opposite hand; return and alternate. Three one-minute blocks: 20 seconds moving, 20 rest and feedback, 20 moving. High knees unnecessary.
## Coaching and safety
Relax shoulders; avoid exaggerated arm crossing. Same-side arm and leg: stop, show opposite position and restart slowly. Raised shoulders: smaller range. No running between pupils or hopping; allow arm clearance and stop for pain or dizziness.
## Adaptations and assessment
Easier: step and pause before changing sides. Harder: walk a clear lane at the same pace, not faster. Stable sitting with adapted arm and foot movements is possible. Check ten steps for coordination and relaxed shoulders. Do not use results as medical diagnosis.
