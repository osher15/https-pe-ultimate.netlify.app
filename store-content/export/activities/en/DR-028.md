## Setup and goal
Ages 11–15; three pupils, sound secured table-tennis table, one bat and ball. Keep 2 m clear at each active side. Goal: after self-feeding, strike gently over net onto opposite half.
## Procedure and timing
7 minutes: 2 demonstration, 4 practice and swaps, 1 feedback and collection. Stand behind end line, release ball by hand for one bounce on own half, then strike forward with forehand. Collector waits to opposite side outside flight path, retrieves after action ends and returns by hand. Observer at side. Three attempts then fixed role rotation. This is self-feeding, not a competitive serve.
## Coaching and safety
Short action, watch ball, bat face towards target. High flight: shorten swing and check angle. No leaning on table, touching net, striking during collection or crawling below active table. Escaped ball: stop station.
## Adaptations and assessment
Easier: no net if teacher can safely remove it before activity. Harder: broad flat-tape target on opposite half, not more force. Stable seated position with suitable leg clearance is possible. Record opposite-half landings, actual attempts and equipment version.
