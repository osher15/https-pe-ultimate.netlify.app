## Setup and goal
Ages 13–17; pair, racket, shuttle and 5×12 m lane. Goal: contact above the hitting shoulder and send forwards. A comfortable grip is required.
## Procedure
Feeder stands diagonally 3 m in front, outside racket reach, and gently tosses an arc above the shoulder. Strike from standing without backward steps. Do not hit a feed behind you. Retrieve after the shuttle lands and racket stops. Take 4 attempts, then swap; up to 2 rounds.
## Safety and adaptations
No jump or maximum-power stroke; nobody in the landing area. Easier: shadow rehearsal. Harder: aim into a broad marked area ahead. A stable seated version needs racket clearance and adapted feeding.
## Timing and assessment
Record contact in front and forward flight in 3 of 4 hittable feeds; unsuitable feeds do not count. Rest while feeding.
Suggested duration: 6 minutes — 1 for demonstration, 4 for turns, retrieval and rests, 1 for feedback. Start after appropriate physical preparation; stop for pain or dizziness.
