## Setup and goal
Ages 9–16; 4–6 participants, light beach ball and place markers in a 4 m diameter circle within a 6×6 m area. Goal: a shared contact sequence involving everyone.
## Procedure, rules and ending
Begin with a gentle toss towards a participant who has shown readiness. Use open palms to send upwards towards a ready participant. No consecutive contacts by the same person. Earn a team point when everyone has touched within an unbroken sequence; catch, reset and restart. A drop, ball out of bounds or double contact ends the sequence; restart without losing earned points. Play 2 rounds of 90 seconds with 30 seconds between, seeking a team best.
## Safety and adaptations
Each person may take one step from their place; let distant balls drop without chasing. No kicks, headers or dives. Check ceiling clearance. Easier: everyone catches and tosses; score that version separately. Stable seated play can use an adapted circle height. Harder: name the receiver before contact, without adding force.
## Timing and assessment
7 minutes: 1 explanation, 1 trial, 3.5 rounds and rest, 1.5 feedback and collection. Record everyone's participation and sequences; do not blame the person nearest a dropped ball.
