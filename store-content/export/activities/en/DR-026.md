## Setup and goal
Ages 10–15; pair and soft football in a 4×4 m area. Performer beside stationary ball, observer 2 m away. Goal: place body between observer and ball without leaning on ball or contacting observer.
## Procedure and timing
6 minutes: 1 demonstration, 4 practice and swaps, 1 feedback. Observer walks slowly in an arc in front, never within 1.5 m and never attempting a steal. Performer scans and uses small steps around ball to keep it on the far side. Stop and swap after 20 seconds; rest as needed.
## Coaching and safety
Stable feet, comfortable knees, arms close to body. No sharp knee twist over a planted foot; change direction with steps. Escaping ball: stop and reset. No pushing, tackling, standing on ball or arm blocking. This is positioning practice, not a duel.
## Adaptations and assessment
Easier: observer stationary, find one position. Harder: two small ball touches while maintaining position, without bringing observer closer. Accessible seated positioning of an object on the far side offers a different perceptual goal. Check three positions for body between ball and observer and safe distance.
