## Identity and setup
Ages 10–15; familiar with stop instructions. Individuals face a stable, clear, dry wall without windows or doors. About 1.5 m clear width per participant; no equipment. If wall space is limited, pair a performer with an observer.
## Observable goal
Complete up to 6 slow repetitions with comfortable body alignment and an exhale during the push, without pain.
## Procedure and timing
5 minutes: 1 demonstration and setup, 3 paired practice, 1 review. Place palms at shoulder height and comfortable width, feet a short distance from the wall. Bend elbows and approach under control, then push back. Perform 4–6 repetitions and swap roles for rest. Up to two sets each if quality allows; do not work to failure.
## Coaching and error correction
Do not force elbows wide; keep the head aligned with the trunk. If the back sags, move closer and reduce range. If breath is held, exhale on the push and count quietly.
## Safety
Check shoe and hand grip; never lean on a movable surface. Shoulder or wrist pain means stop, not more repetitions. No pushing other pupils.
## Adaptations and assessment
Easier: stand closer with smaller range. Harder: slightly farther away only with maintained quality. If standing load-bearing is inaccessible, use unloaded arm reaches from stable sitting as a different movement goal. Assess alignment and breathing over the chosen repetitions; the count is not a fitness grade.
