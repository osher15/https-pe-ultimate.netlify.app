## Setup and goal
Ages 11–16; pair, three soft balls, two cones forming a 2 m goal. Kick line 4 m away; 4 m wide channel and at least 6 m clear beyond goal. Goal: laces-area contact and controlled direction, not maximum power.
## Procedure and timing
7 minutes: 2 demonstration and ball-free preparation, 4 practice and retrieval, 1 feedback. Stationary ball, one approach step, support foot beside ball, kicking toes comfortably downward. Contact centre with instep and follow towards goal. Three kicks; collect and swap only after all balls stop. Observer behind and to side.
## Coaching and safety
No goalkeeper or person beyond kick line. Stable ankle without forcing; no large swing required. Foot pain: stop and review equipment and technique. Excessive loft: reduce force and check contact. No collection during kicks; parallel lanes need separation and coordinated retrieval.
## Adaptations and assessment
Easier: 3 m, no approach step. Harder: 1.5 m goal without more force. Respect individual adaptations; an inside-foot pass is another skill, not evidence of success here. Record contact, balance and direction over three kicks, not ball speed.
