## Setup and goal
Ages 12–17; 6 players in two threes, 6 soft play sticks and foam ball. Field 16×10 m with 2 m margins and a flat 2 m goal centred on each end line. Prior control of stick stopping and pushing required. Goal: open passing lanes and finish with a push.
## Procedure, rules and ending
Walking and short ground pushes only. Score when whole ball crosses opposing goal; no goalkeeper. Defend passing lanes a metre from ball holder, never inserting a stick near feet. After a goal opponents start centrally. Out: boundary restart to opponents of last toucher; if unclear, teacher restores previous possession. Others stay 3 m away at restarts. Direct restart goals do not count; opponents restart from end line. Running, body/stick contact or raised ball: stop and opponents restart at the spot as decided by teacher.
## Safety and adaptations
Stick head below knees; no backswing, striking or stick contact. Two-handed grip where possible. Teacher stops crowding. Adapt grip or mobility only with suitable equipment and safe clearance; without control return to unopposed practice. Ball-only play is a separate alternative with separate scoring.
## Timing and assessment
10 minutes: 2 explanation/trial, 3 play, 1 rest/change ends, 3 play, 1 feedback. Draw starting team; other starts after rest. Scores accumulate; ties stand. Observe passing to space and distance control.
