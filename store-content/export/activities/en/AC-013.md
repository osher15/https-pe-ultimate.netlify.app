## Setup and goal
Ages 12–17; 6 participants labelled A–F, foam ball and tally sheet. A 6×6 m area; 4 play and two observe outside. Goal: identify reception distribution and test a participation rule.
## Procedure, rules and ending
Four players choose recipients for gentle two-handed passes; no travelling with the ball, contact or stealing. Each observer tallies receptions for two coded players. First block: 3 one-minute segments, changing observer pair each minute so everyone observes once and plays twice. Discuss patterns and choose one rule, such as no immediate return to the previous passer. Second block repeats the same three segments and role order using that rule. Stop to collect a dropped ball, then continue without penalty.
## Safety and adaptations
Pass only to ready receivers within comfortable range, never at faces. Use rolling or stable seated play consistently across both blocks. Display codes, not public name rankings. Do not demand equal receptions every minute or blame anyone. Easier: each observer tracks one player; mark data as partial.
## Timing and assessment
12 minutes: 2 explanation, 3 first block, 2 rule choice, 3 second block, 2 reflection. Output: counts per code over their playing minutes and the rule tested. Discuss opportunity changes; two short blocks cannot establish fixed conclusions about a student or rule effectiveness.
