## Setup and goal
Ages 12–16; secure mat, 2×2 m space; for comfortable supine lying. Goal: lift and lower pelvis under control without holding breath.
## Procedure and timing
5 minutes: 1 demonstration, 3 practice and rest, 1 feedback and rising. Knees bent, feet flat comfortably apart. Gently press through feet and lift pelvis slightly; pause one second, lower slowly. Four repetitions, 30 seconds rest; up to two comfortable rounds.
## Coaching and safety
No maximum height, large back arch or pressure on head/neck. Shoulders remain on mat. Pain, cramp or dizziness: stop. No partner pressure or added weight.
## Adaptations and assessment
Easier: smaller range. Harder: three-second lowering, not single-leg. If lying is unsuitable choose another individually adapted skill; no automatic equivalent. Observe breathing, steady pelvis and controlled descent, not maximum repetitions.
