## Setup and goal
Ages 11–16; pair, short racket, foam ball and 5×10 m lane. Feeder stands diagonally 3 m in front. Goal: make brief contact before the ball touches the floor.
## Procedure
Gently toss to the forehand side at waist height. Player places the racket ahead and pushes slightly forward without a large swing. Let the ball land in empty space; retrieve only once the racket stops. Take 4 attempts and swap; up to 2 rounds.
## Safety and adaptations
Feeder stays beyond racket reach and does not catch the return. Do not hit high or rearward feeds. Easier: slower, closer feed while preserving clearance. Harder: direct to a marked area. A stable seated version needs clear racket space.
## Timing and assessment
Target: 3 of 4 contacts before the bounce with a short action. Rest while feeding; do not add force to obtain success.
Suggested duration: 6 minutes — 1 for demonstration, 4 for turns, retrieval and rests, 1 for feedback. Start after appropriate physical preparation; stop for pain or dizziness.
