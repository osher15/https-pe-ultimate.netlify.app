## Setup and goal
Ages 10–15; groups of three. Each group needs three soft beanbags, a flat 1×1 m target, throwing lines 2 and 3 m from its near edge, and a record sheet. Separate 3×7 m lane, clear beyond the target too. Goal: compare results at two distances while changing one variable.
## Procedure
12 minutes: 2 explanation and prediction, 3 trials from 2 m, 3 trials from 3 m, 2 discussion, 2 collection and review. Roles: thrower, recorder, collector. At each distance every pupil makes three one-handed underarm throws; others wait behind the active line. After three throws, stop; collector retrieves with permission and roles rotate until everyone has tried. Keep target, equipment and throwing style identical at both distances.
## Rules and safety
Success: bag comes to rest inside or touching the target boundary. Record 0–3 successes per pupil at each distance; first landing position does not determine the score. Nobody in the target area, no throwing during retrieval and no overarm throws. Sound bags, non-slip surface; stop if anyone enters the lane.
## Adaptations and assessment
Shorten both distances beforehand or throw from stable sitting; keep and record the same adaptation in both tests. Discuss: did results match predictions? What else may affect them? Three attempts do not establish a rule for everyone; fixed order may introduce learning or fatigue effects. Assess accurate recording and cautious explanation, not ability ranking. If time runs short, record actual attempts.
