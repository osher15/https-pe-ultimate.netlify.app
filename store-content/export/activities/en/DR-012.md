## Setup and goal
Ages 8–13; independent standing and walking. Pairs, 3 m floor line with 2 m clear width; observer to the side. No raised beam. Goal: five slow steps with heel approaching the other toes, without a sudden grab.
## Procedure
5 minutes: 1 demonstration, 3 practice, 1 feedback. Place one foot ahead of the other for five steps. Heel and toes need not touch. Walk back normally outside the line and swap roles. Up to three attempts each according to control.
## Coaching and safety
Look ahead, arms free for balance. Wobbling: slightly wider base and slower pace. No closed eyes, pushing, speed challenge or carried objects. One on the line; teacher-approved stable support, not pulling a partner or pupils trying to catch a fall.
## Adaptations and assessment
Easier: ordinary steps in a wider corridor or beside a stable rail. Harder: brief pause each step without height. Seated reaches to markers offer a different goal for non-walking pupils. Record controlled steps and support; do not compare unlike alternatives.
