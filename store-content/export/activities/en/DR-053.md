## Setup and goal
Ages 8–12; pair in 2×10 m lane, 6 m travel and 4 m slowing. Goal: repeating forward step and trailing-foot close with same foot leading.
## Procedure and timing
6 minutes: 2 demonstration, 3 practice and rest, 1 feedback. Lead foot steps forward; other follows behind with a small springing action. Repeat to 6 m line, walk and stop. Return outside before partner starts. Two passes with each lead if comfortable.
## Coaching and safety
No changing lead mid-pass, crossing feet or racing. Low height, soft landing; lost rhythm: stop and return to step-close without flight. Stop for pain or fatigue.
## Adaptations and assessment
Easier: walking step-close. Harder: even rhythm throughout, not faster. Seated rhythm sequence is a different alternative if galloping is inaccessible. Observe four consecutive cycles and maintained lead foot.
