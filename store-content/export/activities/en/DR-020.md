## Setup and goal
Ages 8–13; two clean, light scarves in different colours per pupil, 2×2 m space. Goal: complete two throws and two catches in order. Continuous juggling is not required.
## Procedure and timing
6 minutes: 1 demonstration, 4 practice and rest, 1 feedback and collection. One scarf in each hand. Right hand tosses gently upward towards the left; near its peak, left hand tosses the second upward towards the right. Left catches the first; right catches the second. Stop and reset. After three attempts start with the left, preserving relative order.
## Coaching and safety
Toss slightly above the head, not towards the face. Late second release: practise just the two throws and allow them to fall. No jumping, chasing outside the space, covering faces or tying to bodies. Collect only after stopping; clear scarves from the floor before moving.
## Adaptations and assessment
Easier: separate throw-and-catch with each scarf as preparation. Harder: add another cycle after success without higher or faster throws. Stable seated practice within comfortable reach is possible. In three attempts check release order and visual tracking; reduce demands for fatigue or difficulty seeing.
