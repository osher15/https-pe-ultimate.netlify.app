## Setup and goal
Ages 12–16; trio, secured table, bat and ball; 2 m clear at active sides. Goal: return in front of body with short backhand action, no large swing.
## Procedure and timing
7 minutes: 2 demonstration, 4 practice, 1 feedback. Feeder opposite gently hand-tosses over net for one bounce before performer. Performer presents backhand face and gently directs over net. One attempt then stop and collect; three attempts then rotate. Observer to side.
## Coaching and safety
Do not strike high, fast or distant feeds. No leaning on table or going underneath during play. Bat ahead, elbow not locked; stop for pain.
## Adaptations and assessment
Easier: net removed safely by teacher. Harder: aim at an agreed broad half, not more force. Stable sitting with leg clearance possible. Observe three controlled returns; hand feed is a teaching adaptation, not official serve.
