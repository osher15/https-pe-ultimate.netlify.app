## Setup and goal
Ages 13–17; pair, foam ball, secured catching net and 5×12 m lane. Goal: release while airborne and land with control. Prior throwing and landing competence required.
## Procedure
Start 4 m from the net. Right-handed: step onto the left foot, jump slightly from it and throw gently forward; reverse for left-handed players. Land on the take-off foot, bring down the other and stabilise. Take 3 attempts, retrieving after each, then swap; up to 2 rounds.
## Safety and adaptations
Observer stays behind the start line; no goalkeeper, obstacle or distance contest. No hard throws. Easier: step and throw without jumping; stable seated option. Harder: refine release accuracy without increasing height.
## Timing and assessment
Record airborne release and controlled landing separately. Target: 2 of 3 controlled sequences. Rest during the partner's turn.
Suggested duration: 6 minutes — 1 for demonstration, 4 for turns, retrieval and rests, 1 for feedback. Start after appropriate physical preparation; stop for pain or dizziness.
