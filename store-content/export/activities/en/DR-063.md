## Setup and goal
Ages 9–14; pair, soft football and 3×3 m area. Goal: move the ball across the body with the sole while keeping balance.
## Procedure
Lightly place the sole on top and roll sideways towards the other foot, which stops the ball. Put both feet back on the ground. Make 4 transfers in each direction, then swap with the observer outside the area. Up to 2 rounds.
## Safety and adaptations
Never stand on the ball or press heavily. Easier: shorter roll and longer pause. Harder: stop with the receiving foot without an extra touch. A stable seated version is possible with feet reaching the floor and ball close; do not pull the chair using the ball.
## Timing and assessment
Record 3 of 4 transfers ending within one step without losing balance. Rest during the observer turn.
Suggested duration: 6 minutes — 1 for demonstration, 4 for turns, retrieval and rests, 1 for feedback. Start after appropriate physical preparation; stop for pain or dizziness.
