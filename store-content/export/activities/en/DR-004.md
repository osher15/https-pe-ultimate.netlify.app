## Identity and setup
Ages 9–13; pairs able to catch a soft ball. A 3×5 m rectangle, one soft ball and 3 flat markers per pair. Partners are 3 m apart; bounce marker about 2 m from the passer, adjusted to the ball. For 30: 15 balls and 45 markers.
## Observable goal
Deliver a one-bounce pass between the receiver's waist and chest in 4 of 5 attempts.
## Procedure and timing
6 minutes: 1 demonstration, 4 paired practice, 1 feedback. Hold the ball, check readiness and push with two hands toward the floor marker. Receiver catches after one bounce and returns in the same way. Each pupil has five assessed attempts; remaining time is practice. This is a no-defender accuracy drill, not a goal-shooting lesson.
## Coaching and error correction
Look at the receiver first, release low and use moderate force. If the ball stays too low, move the bounce marker slightly toward the passer or adjust force. If too high, reduce force and check the marker position. Teacher demonstrates using the actual ball's rebound.
## Safety
Dry even surface and a predictably bouncing ball. No face-level passes or passing between pairs. Receiver need not chase an inaccurate pass.
## Adaptations and assessment
Easier: 2 m distance and larger ball. Harder: receiver takes one side-step and stops before the pass. Stable seated participation is possible with adapted distance and receiving height. Count success only after one bounce and controlled reception in the height range; aim for 4/5 before progressing.
