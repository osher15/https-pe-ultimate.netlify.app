## Setup and goal
Ages 11–17; 8 participants in two teams of 4, soft football and flat markers. A 20×20 m area. Ball zone 12 m wide; beside it a 2 m running lane separated by a 2 m empty buffer. Mark a turnaround 5 m from the start. Goal: finish out-and-back before the ball is returned. The return line is beside the kicking line at the same position along the field; receiver is not in front of the kicker. Place the ball near the side of the ball zone closest to the running lane, for a short entry.
## Procedure, rules and ending
One attacker gently kicks a stationary ball along the ground into the ball zone, then enters the running lane and goes out-and-back. Defence has 3 collectors and one receiver near the return line inside the ball zone, away from runners. Collector stops and rolls to another defender, who rolls to the receiver; two different collectors must touch before the receiver. Attack earns a point if runner finishes before receiver controls the ball; ties or doubt favour the runner. Each attacker has one attempt, then teams swap. No individual elimination. Ball out or entering running lane: stop, clear and replay. A missed roll remaining in the ball zone is retrieved and defence continues. Nearest collector calls 'mine'; others stop and give space. If receiver controls first, the attempt ends without a point. Runner slows under control within the lane.
## Safety and adaptations
Never throw at people, tag, steal or chase runners. Waiting attackers stay behind kicking line; defenders stay in ball zone. Stop everyone after each attempt before collection and role changes. Easier: walking roles or nearer turnaround. Adapt with rolling instead of kicking and accessible propulsion lane. Harder: choose open kicking space, not more power.
## Timing and assessment
12 minutes: 2 demonstration, 8 for two halves including collection and swap, 2 review. Up to 4 points per team; draws remain draws. Change defensive receiver each attempt. Assess role sharing and zone separation.
