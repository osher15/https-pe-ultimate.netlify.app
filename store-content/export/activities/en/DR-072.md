## Setup and goal
Ages 12–17; pair, purpose-made soft-tipped foam javelin and marked-off 8×20 m throwing area. Goal: orient the tip forwards during a controlled release.
## Procedure
Stand behind the line with the opposite foot forward. Hold the grip area, tip pointing forwards, and throw gently over the shoulder without a run-up. Take 3 attempts and swap; up to 2 rounds. Everyone waits behind the line; retrieve only on the teacher's signal after all throws stop. After each attempt, wait for the teacher's signal, retrieve and return behind the line before the next throw.
## Safety and adaptations
No rigid javelin or improvised stick. If it leaves the area, stop and inform the teacher. The teacher checks that the area is clear and safe, then adjusts or enlarges the area before restarting. If a clear, safe area cannot be confirmed, do not resume throwing. Easier: short range. Harder: direction within the area, not force. A stable seated version requires arm clearance.
## Timing and assessment
Target: 2 of 3 throws stay in the area with the tip forward at release. No compulsory distance; rest includes retrieval and the partner's turn.
Suggested duration: 6 minutes — 1 for demonstration, 4 for turns, retrieval and rests, 1 for feedback. Start after appropriate physical preparation; stop for pain or dizziness.
