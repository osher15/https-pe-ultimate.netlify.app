## Setup and goal
Ages 10–15; comfortable hand and knee support. Sound mat and 2×2 m square per pupil. Individual adaptations take priority. Goal: four hand slides per side with steady pelvis and continuous breathing.
## Procedure
5 minutes: 1 demonstration, 3 practice and rest, 1 feedback. Hands under shoulders and knees under hips, comfortably. Slide one hand slightly forward on the mat without lifting, return and alternate. Four each side, then rest; up to two rounds if comfortable. Do not lift a leg simultaneously.
## Coaching and safety
Neck aligned with trunk; exhale reaching. Rotating pelvis: shorter slide. Sagging back: reset and reduce range without forced curvature. Wrist, knee or back pain means stop. No partner pressure, breath holding or slippery mat.
## Adaptations and assessment
Easier: small weight shift without sliding. Harder: two-second pause within comfortable range. Stable seated hand slides on a fixed table offer a different goal. Teacher checks pelvis, breathing and comfort; early stopping is not failure.
