## Setup and goal
Ages 9–17; 4 players in two pairs, 8 soft beanbags with two distinct team symbols and a flat target marker, with a soft measuring tape. A 4×8 m lane; target 3 m from the throwing line. Goal: finish closer than the other pair, not throw far.
## Procedure, rules and ending
Pairs alternate underarm throws; alternate within pairs so each player throws 2 bags. No retrieval until all 8 are thrown. Once still, measure distance from each bag's nearest edge to the marker centre; a bag covering the centre has zero distance. Only the closest pair earns one point per round; equal minimum distances earn one each. Displaced bags count in final positions. Bags outside the lane cannot score; if all are outside, no point. Play 3 rounds, alternating starting pair.
## Safety and adaptations
Everyone behind the line during throws. No forceful throws or aiming at people. Collect together only on the teacher's signal. Easier: closer line for the whole pair. Harder: plan the landing spot before release. Stable seated throwing or a sliding ramp may use an adapted line agreed before the round.
## Timing and assessment
9 minutes: 1 explanation, 6 for three rounds including comparison and collection, 2 reflection. Up to 3 points per pair; final ties remain ties. Record force choice and reasoning for adjustments.
