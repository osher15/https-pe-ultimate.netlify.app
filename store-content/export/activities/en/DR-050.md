## Setup and goal
Ages 12–16; trio, short racket, bouncy foam ball, 3×3 m floor target 3 m away; 5×10 m station. Goal: two-handed backhand-side stroke with short forward action.
## Procedure and timing
7 minutes: 2 demonstration, 4 practice, 1 feedback. Feeder stands to side outside racket reach and gently tosses for one bounce at agreed point. Performer holds with both hands and strikes ahead of body towards target. Feeder moves back before strike. One attempt, stop, retrieve; three attempts then rotate.
## Coaching and safety
No large swing, hitting during collection or chasing distant feed. Observer behind outside reach. Stop for hand or back pain; no forced trunk turn.
## Adaptations and assessment
Easier: ball on stable soft tee at comfortable height. Harder: narrower target, no extra force. Seated adaptation requires racket clearance and stability check. Observe three strokes for two-hand grip, contact ahead and direction.
