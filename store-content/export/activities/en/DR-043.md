## Setup and goal
Ages 11–15; trio, soft ball, 6×7 m lane. Feeder beside catch point, 3 m away; receiver starts 2 m before point. Goal: catch and finish balanced.
## Procedure and timing
7 minutes: 2 demonstration, 4 practice, 1 feedback. Receiver walks forward. Feeder gently passes to ready hands; receiver catches, makes a small forward hop and lands on both feet together, holding two seconds. Teacher coordinates slow timing. Three attempts then rotate; observer outside lane.
## Coaching and safety
No running, contested catches or reaching for distant passes. Soften knees; no height target. Instability: return to ball-free practice. Stop for pain. Coordination practice, not a travelling-rule test.
## Adaptations and assessment
Easier: stationary catch then tiny stop without travel. Harder: scan for a target after stopping, without another hop. If hopping is unsuitable, walking catch and controlled halt offer an adapted goal. Observe three controlled catches and stops.
