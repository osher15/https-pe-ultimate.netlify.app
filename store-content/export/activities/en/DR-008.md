## Identity and setup
Ages 6–10; beginners. One light fabric scarf per pupil; a clear 2×2 m personal square. For 30: 30 scarves. No balloons or small objects.
## Observable goal
Toss the scarf just above the head and catch with the other hand inside personal space in 4 of 5 attempts.
## Procedure and timing
5 minutes: 1 demonstration, 3 practice, 1 checking and collection. Gently toss the open scarf with the right hand and catch with the left; reverse hands next attempt. Wait until it lands or is caught before trying again. After each ten attempts, pause for breathing and brief feedback.
## Coaching and error correction
Open the fabric before tossing and track it visually. If it travels sideways, use less force and send it upward. If it folds and falls quickly, reopen it instead of chasing.
## Safety
Do not cover the face or tie it around the neck. Keep the area obstacle-free; no jumping catches or entry into a neighbour's space. Stop before picking up a scarf from the floor.
## Adaptations and assessment
Easier: lower toss and two-hand catch. Harder: tap the knee once with the free hand before catching, without turning or jumping. Stable seated practice with a low toss is possible. Teacher checks five attempts, hand crossing and space control; 4/5 is an editorial target only.
