## Setup and goal
Ages 9–14; securely joined mats in 3×4 m area per active pupil; pair alternates. Goal: slowly roll from back through side to front as a unit without loading neck.
## Procedure and timing
6 minutes: 2 demonstration, 3 practice and rest, 1 feedback and rising. Lie on back, hands near chest. Roll to side then front comfortably, pause and return through side. Two attempts each direction then swap; observer outside mat. Turn head comfortably so face is not pressed down.
## Coaching and safety
No over-head rolling, partner pushing or speed. No mat gaps or raised edges. Stop for dizziness or pain; never force lying or prone position if unsuitable.
## Adaptations and assessment
Easier: back to side only. Harder: link stages slowly and continuously. Seated weight shift may offer a different individualized goal. Observe control, breathing and clear face, not roll count.
