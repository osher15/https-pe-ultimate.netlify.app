## Setup and goal
Ages 11–16; pair, badminton racket and shuttle, 3×8 m lane. 2×2 m target begins 3 m away. Goal: release and contact below waist towards target; no net or forward receiver.
## Procedure and timing
6 minutes: 1 demonstration, 4 practice and retrieval, 1 feedback. Hold shuttle ahead with free hand, release and make short underarm forward stroke. Wait for landing and put racket down before collection. Three attempts then swap; observer behind and to side.
## Coaching and safety
Racket stays low; no overhead swing, hard whip or person within reach. Poor release: stop and reset. Educational opening stroke, not official service-law assessment.
## Adaptations and assessment
Easier: target at 2 m. Harder: narrower target at same distance. Stable sitting with approved racket clearance possible. Observe three controlled contacts and consistent release, not power.
