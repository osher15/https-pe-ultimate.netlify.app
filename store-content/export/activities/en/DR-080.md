## Setup and goal
Ages 8–14; pair, 4 soft beanbags and a flat 1×1 m target 3 m away, within a 3×8 m lane. Goal: adjust an underarm release to land on target.
## Procedure
Hold beside the thigh, swing comfortably backwards and forwards, step with the opposite foot and release in a low arc. Throw all 4 bags, then collect together at a walk. Swap; up to 2 rounds.
## Safety and adaptations
Observer behind the throwing line; nobody in the target or flight area. Bags must be sealed with no hard parts. Easier: closer or without a step. Harder: smaller target, not a higher throw. Stable seated option with adjusted target.
## Timing and assessment
Record target landings alongside opposite-foot stepping; suggested target: 3 of 4 controlled releases. Rest during retrieval and observation.
Suggested duration: 6 minutes — 1 for demonstration, 4 for turns, retrieval and rests, 1 for feedback. Start after appropriate physical preparation; stop for pain or dizziness.
