## Setup and goal
Ages 9–15; comfortable standing, stable wall or rail for hand support, 1 m width each. No step or weights. Goal: four to six small rises onto both forefeet and slow lowering without pain or ankle rolling.
## Procedure
5 minutes: 1 demonstration, 3 practice and rest, 1 feedback. Parallel feet at comfortable width; touch support. Raise heels slightly and lower to floor under control. Four to six repetitions, at least 30 seconds rest; up to two comfortable rounds. No bouncing or maximum-height target.
## Coaching and safety
Pressure across forefoot, not only edges. Swaying: smaller range and more support. Abrupt lowering: slow down. Non-slip shoes and surface; no partner or movable support. Foot, ankle or calf pain means stop.
## Adaptations and assessment
Easier: stable seated heel lifts at a different load. Harder: two-second pause at top, without switching to one leg. Record lowering control and comfortable repetitions; do not rank strength by count.
