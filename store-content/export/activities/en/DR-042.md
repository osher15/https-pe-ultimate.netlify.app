## Setup and goal
Ages 12–16; pair, basketball, 3×6 m lane with 1 m retreat segment. Goal: move ball away from imaginary pressure while controlled; no active opponent.
## Procedure and timing
6 minutes: 2 demonstration, 3 practice and rest, 1 feedback. Check behind for clear space, dribble beside body and take two small backward steps within segment. Stop and gather. Four attempts then swap; observer outside segment to side.
## Coaching and safety
No backward running, crossed feet or defender. Keep bounce beside leg. Observer calls stop if anything enters lane. Without checking or stability, return to stationary practice.
## Adaptations and assessment
Easier: one backward step. Harder: after stopping, pass forward to ready receiver. If backward travel is unsuitable, stationary side dribbling is a different alternative. Observe scanning, two small steps and control in four attempts.
