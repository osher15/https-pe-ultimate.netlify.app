## Setup and goal
Ages 9–14; pair, continuous secure 2×6 m mat lane with 3 m travel segment. Goal: move on hands and knees without rushing or collapsing trunk.
## Procedure and timing
6 minutes: 1 demonstration, 4 practice and rest, 1 feedback and rising. From all fours move a hand slightly, then a knee, incorporating other side at comfortable pace. No required opposite-limb pattern initially. Travel 3 m, stop and rise gradually. Two passes with at least 30 seconds rest; observer outside.
## Coaching and safety
Look slightly ahead and down, hands not far from shoulders. No race, extra load or crawling under someone. Non-slip mats; stop for wrist or knee pain.
## Adaptations and assessment
Easier: small weight shifts without travel. Harder: small opposite-arm/leg steps without acceleration. Seated weight shifts offer a different alternative if floor support inaccessible. Observe continuous support and breathing along route.
