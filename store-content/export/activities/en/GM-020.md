## Setup and goal
Ages 11–17; 8 players in two fours, 3 marked beanbags per team. A 20×12 m area split into halves. A 2×2 m base at each end and clear 2 m outer margins. Goal: bring an original opposing bag to your own base.
## Procedure, rules and ending
Everyone walks. Carry one bag only; no passing or throwing. Tag opponents only in your own half with a light upper-arm tap; no tagging inside bases. Tagged without a bag: return home with a raised hand, without interfering, then rejoin. With a bag: first return it to its source base, then walk home under the same immunity. No tagging during this return. Stay in opposing base at most 5 seconds, one attacker at a time; waiting attackers stay outside and can be tagged. Delivered bags earn a point and are banked separately, never stolen back. Round ends at 3 minutes or transfer of all 3 bags; reset and change ends.
## Safety and adaptations
No clothing grabs, body blocking, pushing or contact with heads or chairs. Leaving bounds or staying over 5 seconds in the opposing base counts as being tagged. If a bag drops, its carrier stops to pick it up; no snatching from hands or floor, and the carrier may be tagged under the rules while picking up. Stop disputes for teacher resolution without punishment. Agree beforehand whether light contact suits everyone; otherwise whole group uses cooperative collection without tags or competitive scores. Use wide accessible routes and controlled walking or propulsion; teacher stops base crowding.
## Timing and assessment
10 minutes: 2 explanation and trial, up to 3 round, 1 rest/reset, up to 3 round, 1 feedback. Use time saved by early finish for rest and planning. Scores accumulate; ties stand. Share attacking and defending initiatives; no prisoners or waiting for rescue.
