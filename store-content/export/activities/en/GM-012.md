## Setup and goal
Ages 10–16; 4 participants, soft football and 6 stable foam cylinders. A 4×10 m lane; targets 4 m beyond the kicking line with empty space behind. Goal: knock down targets together within limited attempts.
## Procedure, rules and ending
Each participant kicks once per round, using the inside of the foot with moderate force. After each kick everyone waits for the ball to stop; kicker retrieves on the teacher's signal. Leave fallen targets down until round end. After 4 kicks count distinct targets toppled and reset. Play 3 rounds, trying to improve the total up to 6 per round. A miss or out-of-bounds ball counts as an attempt; retrieve walking. Stop an unauthorised early kick and revisit instructions, without physical punishment.
## Safety and adaptations
Wait behind the line; no goalkeeper or person beside targets. Retrieve only when adjacent lanes also have no moving balls. Easier: nearer line. Harder: announce a chosen target, without bonus. Alternative: hand roll or stable ramp, same number of attempts and adapted line; record adaptation.
## Timing and assessment
9 minutes: 1 explanation, 6 for the three rounds including collection and resetting, 2 feedback. If time is short, finish after a complete round rather than rushing collection. Record team score and everyone's contribution, without individual ranking.
