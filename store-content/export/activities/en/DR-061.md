## Setup and goal
Ages 11–16; pair, foam ball and flat centre marker in a 4×4 m area. Goal: time a catch above the forehead and land under control. Comfortable two-foot landing is a prerequisite.
## Procedure
Toss just above standing reach, jump slightly and catch with both hands. Land, hold for 2 seconds and lower to the chest. Take 4 attempts, then swap with the observer outside the area. Up to 2 rounds, resting 30 seconds before another round.
## Safety and adaptations
No basket, opponent or contact. Retrieve a stray ball after landing; never leap after it. Easier: standing catch. Harder: improve timing at the same height. From a stable seat, practise a comfortable high catch without a landing goal.
## Timing and assessment
Record catching and balance separately; suggested target: 3 of 4 controlled attempts, without seeking maximum height.
Suggested duration: 6 minutes — 1 for demonstration, 4 for turns, retrieval and rests, 1 for feedback. Start after appropriate physical preparation; stop for pain or dizziness.
