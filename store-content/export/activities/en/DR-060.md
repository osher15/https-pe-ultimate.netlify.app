## Setup and goal
Ages 10–15; pair, two foam balls, 3×5 m lane. Face each other standing or seated 2 m apart. Two separate rolling tracks marked with flat strips. Goal: release one ball and receive another without collisions.
## Procedure and timing
6 minutes: 2 demonstration, 3 practice and rest, 1 feedback and collection. Each holds ball in right hand. Shared signal: both roll along their right-hand track and prepare left to stop incoming ball. Fully stop both, transfer each ball to right and reset. Four exchanges then 20 seconds rest.
## Coaching and safety
No airborne throws or new exchange before both balls stop. Collision: stop and widen track separation. No chasing; retrieve outside lane after both stop. Seated players remain within comfortable reach.
## Adaptations and assessment
Easier: roll one after another. Harder: change releasing hand and tracks after stopping and agreeing. If two-hand use is inaccessible, sequential exchange with available hand is an adapted goal. Observe four exchanges and shared start signal, not speed.
