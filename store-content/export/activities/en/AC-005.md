## Setup and goal
Ages 10–15; four pupils, foam ball, flat 1×2 m target rectangle and rolling line 2 m away. Station 3×6 m. Goal: practise observation, reasoned decisions and calm restarts using an agreed simple rule.
## Procedure and timing
12 minutes: 2 explanation and demonstration, 6 practice, 2 discussion, 2 retry and collection. Rule: ball resting inside or touching rectangle boundary is in; merely rolling through is insufficient. Roles each turn: roller, official, rule explainer and collector. Once ball stops, official says “in” or “out” and states what was observed; explainer identifies relevant rule. Collector returns only after decision ends and area is clear. Rotate after two rolls.
## Agreement and safety
Unclear position means replay without a point. This is the chosen practice rule, not an official sport rule. No personal arguments or ridicule; each pupil may request one explanation. Roller stays behind line; others to sides outside ball path. No kicking or airborne throwing. Stop signal cancels roll.
## Adaptations and assessment
Use seated rolling, visible ball, contrasting boundary, or spoken/card decisions. Teacher may adapt distance without changing decision rule. Easier: ball stops clearly away from boundary. Harder: discuss a close boundary case without moving ball before decision. Everyone explains a decision and performs a calm restart; no competitive scoring.
