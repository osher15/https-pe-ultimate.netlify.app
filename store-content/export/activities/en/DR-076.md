## Setup and goal
Ages 12–17; pair, stable wall and 2×3 m space. Goal: controlled lowering and rising with feet staying in place, without weights.
## Procedure
Stand beside the wall with a hand touching it, one foot forward and the other back at comfortable width. Bend both knees slightly, lower vertically and rise. Take 4 repetitions per side, then swap with the observer; up to 2 rounds.
## Safety and adaptations
Front heel stays grounded; knee tracks with the foot. No compulsory depth or knee-to-floor contact. Easier: tiny range and stable support. Harder: lower for 3 seconds, not deeper. If standing is unsuitable, choose a separate seated exercise.
## Timing and assessment
Target: 3 of 4 controlled, pain-free repetitions per side. Rest at least 30 seconds before repeating the same side, including observation time.
Suggested duration: 6 minutes — 1 for demonstration, 4 for turns, retrieval and rests, 1 for feedback. Start after appropriate physical preparation; stop for pain or dizziness.
