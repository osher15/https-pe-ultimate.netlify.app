## Setup and goal
Ages 11–17; 4 participants, two soft balls of different sizes, paper and pencil. A 4×6 m area; passer and receiver 3 m apart, two recorders to the side. Goal: choose equipment through experience and describe differences.
## Procedure, rules and ending
Label balls A and B. Use the same gentle two-handed pass and distance. Every minute rotate passer, receiver, catch recorder and comfort recorder. Each passer attempts up to 3 passes; never rush retrieval to meet a quota. After a 4-minute role cycle, change ball and repeat. With several groups, some start A and others B. Finally everyone chooses a ball and explains why.
## Safety and adaptations
Pass only to a ready receiver, never at the face. Stop to retrieve stray balls. Both balls must be intact, soft and light; no heavy ball needed. Reduce distance equally for both or use stable seating. Record any adaptation that changes between trials rather than presenting them as identical comparisons.
## Timing and assessment
12 minutes: 2 explanation and labelling, 4 first ball, 4 second ball, 2 discussion. Record catches out of actual attempts and personal comfort. Size, mass and texture may change together, so do not attribute differences to size alone. No single 'winning ball' for every student.
