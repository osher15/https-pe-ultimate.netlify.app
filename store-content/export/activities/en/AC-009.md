## Setup and goal
Ages 10–17; groups of 4, foam ball, paper and pencil. A 4×6 m space; passer and receiver 3 m apart, two recorders outside the passing line. Goal: design and test clear communication, not fast passing totals.
## Procedure, rules and ending
Agree on two distinct signals: 'ready to receive' and 'wait'; write their meanings. Receiver signals; passer gently passes only after readiness, holding on a wait signal. After catching, return gently and start another trial. If unclear, do not pass; ask. Every minute rotate through passer, receiver, decision recorder and readiness recorder. After four minutes improve an unclear signal and repeat a full role cycle.
## Safety and adaptations
Never pass at faces or to someone looking away. Retrieve stray balls after stopping. Raised palm and 'stop' remain an emergency signal, never another code. Use a spoken code or large card to meet access needs; stable seated passing is possible. Easier: word and gesture together. Harder: gesture only if accessible to everyone.
## Timing and assessment
12 minutes: 2 design, 4 testing and rotations, 1 revision, 4 retest, 1 summary. For each trial record whether pass/wait matched the signal and whether the receiver was ready. Output: two agreed signals and a reason for revision; a small improvement does not establish general effectiveness.
