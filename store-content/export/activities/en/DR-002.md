## Identity and setup
Ages 8–12; pairs who can stop a ball. A 3×6 m rectangle, one soft ball and 4 cones per pair. Two cones form a 1 m gate centrally; partners stand 4 m apart. For 30 pupils: 15 balls and 60 cones.
## Observable goal
Send a ground pass through the gate and control the return near the foot in 4 of 5 pass–receive cycles.
## Procedure and timing
6 minutes: 1 demonstration, 4 passing, 1 checking and collection. Stop the ball, look at the receiver and pass through the gate with the inside of the foot. Receiver stops it before returning it. After five passes each, change from the preferred foot to the other as ability allows. No speed contest.
## Coaching and error correction
Supporting foot beside the ball and pointing toward the gate; firm passing ankle and moderate swing. If the ball lifts, reduce force, contact its centre and avoid leaning back. If the first touch escapes, soften contact and reduce distance.
## Safety
Ground passes only; no hard shots or sliding. Pair lanes must not cross. Do not chase a ball into another area; wait until clear and walk to retrieve.
## Adaptations and assessment
Easier: 1.5 m gate and 3 m separation. Harder: 0.75 m gate at the original distance, only after control. For motor access use a larger slower ball or a hand roll as a separate accuracy goal. Partner records five cycles per pupil; both a gate pass and a controlled return are needed for success.
