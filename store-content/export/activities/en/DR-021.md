## Setup and goal
Ages 9–14; pair in a 3×6 m lane. Short light racket, foam ball and two flat gate markers 1 m apart, 2 m from starting line. Goal: send the ball along the floor through the gate in three of five attempts.
## Procedure and timing
6 minutes: 1 demonstration, 4 practice and swaps, 1 feedback and collection. Place a stationary ball ahead and slightly to the racket-hand side. Bring racket face close and push forward briefly to roll it; no backswing. Partner waits behind and to the side, retrieves after ball and racket stop, and returns by hand. Swap after five attempts.
## Coaching and safety
Face points towards gate and stays near floor; bend knees comfortably without forcing a deep back bend. Ball lifts: reduce force and adjust contact angle. No hard hitting, swings above waist, goalkeeper or collection during movement. Keep space beyond gate clear.
## Adaptations and assessment
Easier: 1.5 m gate. Harder: 0.75 m gate without more force. If floor reach is uncomfortable, use suitable handle and teacher-approved accessible position; no forced leaning from a chair. Observe direction, rolling and racket control; changed target size is not a new exercise.
