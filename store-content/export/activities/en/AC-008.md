## Setup and goal
Ages 11–16; four pupils in 6×8 m area, foam ball, four flat markers and teacher-written checklist: boundaries, waiting, passing path, retrieval, stop signal. Goal: set up a clearly usable station and check safe operation. Teacher checks area and equipment beforehand; never plant hazards for the activity.
## Procedure and timing
14 minutes: 2 explanation, 3 setup, 4 movement check, 3 revision and retest, 2 review and collection. Mark passing spots 3 m apart and two waiting spots behind and to the side, outside path. Initial responsibilities: boundaries, equipment, instructions, recording. After teacher approval, two make three gentle passes while others observe, then swap pairs. Practise stop signal; after placing ball on floor, demonstrate approved retrieval.
## Coaching and safety
After stopping ask: did everyone know when to pass and who collects? Did waiting players remain clear? Never throw deliberately towards obstacles or move heavy equipment. Only teacher authorizes restart. Finding difficulty is not failure; fix before retrying.
## Adaptations and assessment
Easier: teacher supplies positions, group checks and explains. Harder: adapt distance and waiting spots for seated participation without narrowing clear route. Rolling and accessible-height passes are possible. Everyone checks and performs. Success: each explains stop/retrieval instructions and group records one change or why none was needed. Standalone organization activity, not a lesson plan.
