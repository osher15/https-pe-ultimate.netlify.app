## Setup and goal
Ages 9–14; two players, foam ball and 3×6 m court. Central neutral strip 1 m deep; each half 2.5 m deep. Another 2 m clear behind each end line. Goal: roll beyond opponent's end line while protecting yours.
## Procedure and timing
10 minutes: 2 explanation, 3 play, 1 rest, 3 play, 1 review. Draw lots to start. Roll by hand from own half when opponent is ready; may stop with hands then roll back within 5 seconds. Score when whole ball crosses end line before being stopped; conceding player restarts from own half. Side exit or airborne ball: no point, opponent of last roller restarts. Sidelines count in. If the ball stops before reaching the opponent's half, no point is awarded and the opponent of the last roller restarts. If a player holds the ball for more than 5 seconds, no point is awarded and that player's opponent restarts. Enter the neutral strip only to retrieve after both players stop; return to positions before restarting. At five points reset score and continue until finish signal.
## Coaching and safety
During play, no entering neutral strip, kicking, diving, sliding or deliberate body blocking. Side steps allowed; do not chase after ball crosses end. Stop and retrieve when clear. Unsafe force stops play and is reduced; no demand for a quick return outside comfortable reach.
## Adaptations and assessment
Easier: 2 m end-line width. Harder: vary rolling angles without more force. Stable seated play can use a line within arm reach and mutually agreed common width. Observe direction, stopping and fairness; no elimination after a point.
