## Setup and goal
Ages 8–13; sound, appropriately sized rope per performer and clear 3×3 m square, including overhead space. In pairs, observer stays outside; one rope per pair. Goal: five controlled turn–stop–step cycles without requiring a jump.
## Procedure and timing
6 minutes: 1 demonstration, 4 practice and rest, 1 feedback and collection. Start with rope behind heels. Turn it forward overhead until it lies on the floor ahead of the feet. Stop the turn, step over with one foot then the other. Reset; five cycles then swap. This prepares for skipping; it is not continuous skipping.
## Coaching and safety
Comfortably relaxed grip and slow, symmetrical turn. Snagged rope: stop and ask teacher to adjust length; do not pull hard. Never tie rope to body or partner, swing near others or run dragging it. Stop for pain or lost stability.
## Adaptations and assessment
Easier: step over a rope already on the floor. Harder: five cycles with less resetting time, without rushing or automatically adding jumps. If stepping is inaccessible, seated handle movements without rope use a different coordination goal. Check that rope stops before stepping and feet remain untangled.
