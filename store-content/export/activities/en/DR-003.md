## Identity and setup
Ages 10–14; beginners. Pairs in a 3×5 m rectangle; one light soft ball and 3 markers per pair. Feeder stands 2 m away; receiver is between two markers 1 m apart. For 30: 15 balls and 45 markers.
## Observable goal
Move behind an easy feed and direct a catchable forearm pass to the feeder in 4 of 5 attempts.
## Procedure and timing
6 minutes: 1 demonstration, 4 practice, 1 feedback. Feeder announces left or right in advance and gently underarm-tosses about half a metre to that side of the receiver. Receiver side-steps, settles and returns with the forearms; feeder catches. Swap roles every five feeds. No net.
## Coaching and error correction
Join hands into a level forearm platform; angle it toward the feeder with a small leg action. A sideways rebound calls for earlier positioning. An excessive arm swing calls for a smaller movement and gentler feed.
## Safety
Soft feeds below head height and within one step only. No diving or chasing into another pair's area. Clear stray balls before the next attempt. Stop for forearm pain and adapt the ball.
## Adaptations and assessment
Easier: straight feed without travel and a lighter ball. Harder: announce the side as the ball is fed, retaining the small range. Stable seated practice with a central feed can target the forearm platform. Record five returns per pupil; below 4/5 reduce travel, not increase force.
