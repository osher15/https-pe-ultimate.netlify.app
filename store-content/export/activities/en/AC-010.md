## Setup and goal
Ages 10–17; 4–12 participants, flat markers, timer and personal sheet. A 10×10 m square route with clear 2 m margins. Goal: choose and adjust pace according to comfort and control, without fitness comparisons.
## Procedure, rules and ending
Everyone walks the same direction, starting 3 m apart. First walk for 2 minutes at a comfortable pace; privately label it easy, suitable or strenuous. Rest one minute and choose to maintain or slow down. Walk a second 2-minute round and check the choice. After one minute's rest, complete a third 2-minute round at the chosen pace and describe what helped sustain it. No lap, speed or distance target.
## Safety and adaptations
No running or overtaking; slow down if the gap closes, never push the person ahead. Stop for pain, dizziness or unusual breathlessness and tell the teacher. Resting out is allowed, with re-entry at the entrance on approval. Adapt with a wheelchair-accessible route or shorter duration; progress does not require more effort. Check weather and surface beforehand.
## Timing and assessment
12 minutes: 2 explanation, 2 walking, 1 rest, 2 walking, 1 rest, 2 walking, 2 reflection. Personal output: selected pace, adjustment and reason. Perceived effort is not a medical measure or fitness test; share only what feels comfortable.
