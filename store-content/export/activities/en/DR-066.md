## Setup and goal
Ages 12–17; pair, soft volleyball and 4×10 m lane. Goal: open-palm contact above and in front of the head, directing the ball downwards. Mark a 1×1 m floor target 2 m away.
## Procedure
Toss slightly in front of the hitting shoulder, contact high within comfortable reach and gently direct towards the target. Wait for the ball to stop, retrieve and reset. Take 4 attempts, then swap with the observer behind; up to 2 rounds.
## Safety and adaptations
No net, jump or receiver in front. Avoid excessive back arch and forceful hits. Easier: rehearse the arm action without a ball. Harder: consistent contact, not power. A stable seated version uses an adjusted toss height.
## Timing and assessment
Count target hits separately from open-palm contact. Suggested target: 3 of 4 controlled contacts. Rest while observing.
Suggested duration: 6 minutes — 1 for demonstration, 4 for turns, retrieval and rests, 1 for feedback. Start after appropriate physical preparation; stop for pain or dizziness.
