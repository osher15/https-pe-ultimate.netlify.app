## Setup and goal
Ages 10–14; pair in a 3×8 m lane, two light balls and a 2×2 m target starting 3 m from the service line. No net or receiver ahead; observer behind the line to the side. Number of separated lanes depends on available space. Goal: first landing in target in 3 of 5 attempts.
## Procedure
7 minutes: 2 demonstration and ball-free rehearsal, 4 practice, 1 feedback and collection. Hold ball in non-striking hand in front of hips, foot opposite striking hand forward. Release just before contact and swing hand from below forward into the lower rear of the ball. Two attempts, stop lane and collect on signal; swap performer. Never serve during collection.
## Coaching and safety
Small swing, firm contact. High toss: small release again. Sideways ball: align body and hand to target. Excess distance: bring target closer rather than hit harder. Spare balls to the side, not underfoot. No one in landing zone; stop for hand or shoulder pain.
## Adaptations and assessment
Easier: target starts 2 m away, lighter ball. Harder: starts 4 m away after accuracy, retaining clear run-out behind. Stable seated practice with adapted range is possible. Target lines count in. Record up to five personal attempts and actual count if fewer occur.
