## Setup and goal
Ages 12–16; trio, three light volleyballs and 4×12 m lane. Start line and flat 3×3 m target beginning 4 m away; no net or receiver. Goal: consistent release and moderate open-palm contact above shoulder height.
## Procedure and timing
7 minutes: 2 demonstration, 4 practice and retrieval, 1 feedback. Foot opposite hitting hand slightly forward. Release ball slightly above intended contact height and ahead of hitting shoulder; strike with open palm within comfortable reach. Poor release: let ball drop and reset. Three attempts, stop, authorized collection and performer swap; others behind and to side.
## Coaching and safety
No jumping, excessive back arch or hitting behind head. Stop for shoulder or hand pain. Ball out of reach: adjust release, do not chase. Nobody moves ahead until all balls stop; all balls travel same direction.
## Adaptations and assessment
Easier: release only without hitting. Harder: smaller target at same distance, not more force. Stable seated version only within comfortable range and individual adaptation; underarm serve is a different-skill alternative. Observe three controlled releases and contacts; target landing is extra feedback, never a reason to progress through pain.
