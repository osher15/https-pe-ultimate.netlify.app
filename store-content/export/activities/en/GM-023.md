## Setup and goal
Ages 8–15; 6 participants in a 6 m diameter circle within a clear 8×8 m area. Goal: identify a leader through observation and timing. Agree on three comfortable actions: stepping in place, clapping and elbow bends/extensions.
## Procedure, rules and ending
One observer briefly faces away standing, with eyes uncovered. Teacher silently selects a leader among the other five. Observer watches from outside; all copy the leader, who changes action at least twice per round. Up to 2 guesses in a minute: everyone stops at a guess and teacher confirms or rejects. Correct guess, two incorrect guesses or time expiry ends the round; reveal and rotate. Play 6 rounds so each observes once, without elimination or punishment.
## Safety and adaptations
All stay in place; only observer may walk a little outside, without approaching or touching. No sudden movements. Use seated actions with heel lifts replacing steps. Easier: leader changes on teacher cue. Harder: explain which clue helped, not faster actions.
## Timing and assessment
10 minutes: 2 explanation/trial, up to 6 rounds, 1 changes, 1 reflection. Early-finish time goes to rest and feedback. Record movement cues used, without ranking deception ability.
