## Setup and goal
Ages 10–15; pair, soft-bladed play stick, foam ball, three flat markers 2 m apart; 3×10 m lane. Goal: guide alternating directions without lifting blade.
## Procedure and timing
6 minutes: 1 demonstration, 4 practice and rest, 1 feedback. Start 1 m before first marker, walk zigzag around all three with small touches. Stop beyond last and carry ball back outside. Two passes then swap; observer behind.
## Coaching and safety
Blade near floor, two hands on stick. No hitting, tackling or observer within reach. Escaping ball: stop and reset at a walk. No improvised rigid stick.
## Adaptations and assessment
Easier: straight route. Harder: brief forward looks between touches, not faster. Mobile seated version needs checked wide route and suitable support; never require unsafe simultaneous propulsion and handling. Observe two passes with ball within control.
