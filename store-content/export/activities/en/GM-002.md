## Identity and setup
Ages 11–15; safe movement, passing and catching. 4v4 on a 12×18 m court with a 2 m-deep end zone at each end. One soft ball, 8 bibs and 8 cones. Additional groups need separate courts and safety gaps; do not put a whole class on this court.
## Observable goal
Create an open passing line and catch under control in the end zone, involving different receivers without contact.
## Procedure and timing
10 minutes: 2 explanation, 3 play, 1 feedback and rest, 3 play, 1 review. Start with a pass from the centre. Ball carrier remains stationary for up to 5 seconds; others move into space. Score one point for an attacking catch inside the target end zone. Before scoring, complete three passes to three different receivers in the same possession. After a point the opponents restart at halfway. A drop, out-of-bounds or time violation gives opponents a restart at that location; if inside an end zone, restart at its front line. No self-passing. Intercept airborne passes only.
## Coaching and error correction
Show hands and move into a passing lane, not toward the carrier. If everyone crowds together, pause and spread across the width. Shorten overpowered passes. Teacher initially counts the five seconds aloud.
## Safety
No contact, body blocking or jumping into a player. Keep at least an arm's length from the carrier. No taking the ball from hands. Stop for an unrelated ball on court and before fatigue compromises control.
## Adaptations and assessment
Easier: 3v3, two passes to two different receivers and a wide end zone. Harder: a point counts only after a pass across the width during the possession. For access, agree a longer passing window and accessible space beforehand, applying rules to both teams. No elimination; ties allowed. Observe three possessions for passing lanes, involvement and spacing, not just winning.
