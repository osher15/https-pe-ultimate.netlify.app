## Setup and goal
Ages 9–14; pair, soft ball manageable with both hands, 3×6 m channel. Face each other 3 m apart. Goal: direct chest-height pass caught without moving in four of five attempts.
## Procedure and timing
6 minutes: 1 demonstration, 4 practice and pauses, 1 feedback and collection. Ball in front of chest, elbows comfortably bent. When receiver shows ready hands, push forward with both hands and release; a short forward step is optional. Receiver bends elbows to cushion, then passes back. Pause for feedback after five passes each.
## Coaching and safety
Ball rises towards face: aim lower and reduce force. Sideways pass: check both hands finish towards receiver. No aiming at the body, passing to an unready partner or chasing into another channel. Dropped ball: both stop, retrieve at a walk.
## Adaptations and assessment
Easier: 2 m and lighter ball. Harder: 4 m only while retaining a gentle pass. Stable sitting and adapted height are possible. Observe direction and symmetrical release; record distance and actual attempts.
