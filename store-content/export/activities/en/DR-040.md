## Setup and goal
Ages 10–15; no equipment, 2×2 m per pupil. Goal: alternate arms forward and back without shrugging or large trunk rotation.
## Procedure and timing
5 minutes: 1 demonstration, 3 practice and rest, 1 feedback. Comfortable stance, one foot slightly forward if comfortable. Elbows bent, hands relaxed. Move one arm forward and other back with a small easy action. Three rounds of 15 seconds movement and 30 seconds rest; remaining practice time for setup and individual feedback.
## Coaching and safety
Movement comes from shoulder, not repeated elbow opening and closing alone. No forced elbow angle, clenched fists or wide swing. Rising shoulders: slow down and shorten action. No weights or bands; stop for pain or dizziness.
## Adaptations and assessment
Easier: one arm at a time, other resting. Harder: combine with slow walking in a clear personal lane, no sprinting. Stable sitting with suitable support is possible. Observe alternating arms, relaxed shoulders and steady trunk. Focus is arm action; arm–leg coordination is a separate goal.
