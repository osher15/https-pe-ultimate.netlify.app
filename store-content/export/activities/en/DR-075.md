## Setup and goal
Ages 10–16; pair, 6 flat markers and 2×16 m lane: 4 m approach, 6 m practice and 6 m slowing zone. Goal: even rhythm without forced stride length.
## Procedure
Start at an easy run. Teacher spaces markers to natural steps within the practice zone. Place one step in each gap, continue into the slowing zone and return outside the lane. Take 3 passes and swap; up to 2 rounds.
## Safety and adaptations
No rigid ladders or speed contest. Start only when clear; change unsuitable spacing rather than forcing longer steps. Easier: rhythmic walking. Harder: less downward looking. Wheelchair option: rhythmic pushes without a step requirement.
## Timing and assessment
Observer checks continuity without skips or stops between markers in 2 of 3 passes. Rest on the walk back and while observing.
Suggested duration: 6 minutes — 1 for demonstration, 4 for turns, retrieval and rests, 1 for feedback. Start after appropriate physical preparation; stop for pain or dizziness.
