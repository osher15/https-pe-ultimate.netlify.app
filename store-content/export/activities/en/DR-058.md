## Setup and goal
Ages 11–16; stable wall, 1 m width each. Goal: mild calf stretch with rear heel down and relaxed breathing.
## Procedure and timing
5 minutes: 1 demonstration, 3 practice and adjustment, 1 feedback. Hands on wall, one foot behind, both feet pointing forward. Slightly bend front knee until mild rear-calf sensation; rear knee comfortably straight, not locked. Hold up to 15 seconds, release and swap. Up to two rounds with pauses.
## Coaching and safety
No bouncing, pain or partner pushing. Raised heel or arched back: shorten stance and reduce lean. Dry surface and secure wall. Tendon or joint pain means stop.
## Adaptations and assessment
Easier: shorter stance and hold. Harder means maintaining stability and breathing without reminders, not stronger stretch. Seated ankle movement offers a different goal if standing unsuitable. Record comfort and stability, not maximum range.
