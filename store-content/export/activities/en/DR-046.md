## Setup and goal
Ages 11–16; trio, ball, two markers in 6×8 m area. Starter centrally on short end; partner 3 m ahead and 2 m sideways. Return point 2 m ahead of start. Goal: pass and move in time for return.
## Procedure and timing
7 minutes: 2 explanation, 4 practice, 1 feedback. Pass by foot to partner, walk to return point. Partner stops ball, looks up and returns only when receiver is ready. Receive and stop. Three sequences then fixed rotation; observer outside passing triangle.
## Coaching and safety
Gentle ground passes, no opponent or running. One-touch passing not required. Early timing: partner waits instead of hurrying receiver. Stray ball: everyone stops before retrieval.
## Adaptations and assessment
Easier: arrive and stop before return. Harder: return during continuous slow walking. Use only accessible passing and travel; hand-roll version on accessible route offers a different combined goal. Observe pass, movement and readiness over three sequences.
