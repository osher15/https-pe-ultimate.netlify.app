## Setup and goal
Ages 11–17; 4 participants, foam ball, 4 markers and recording sheet. A 5×5 m space. Goal: practise stopping, factual description and agreed restart without deciding who is 'to blame'.
## Procedure, rules and ending
Two participants roll back and forth through a metre-wide marked gate; a third facilitates and fourth records. After 3 rolls the teacher introduces a fictional situation: unclear whether the ball passed inside the gate. No real dispute is needed. Stop; each describes what they saw in one sentence; facilitator proposes an agreed restart without changing a score, such as replay. Complete 3 more rolls under that agreement. Rotate every 2 minutes so everyone tries all four roles. If no agreement, teacher sets a neutral restart and play resumes.
## Safety and adaptations
Do not reenact pushing, insults or physical conflict. No forced apology or public personal discussion. Anyone may request a break; teacher supports wording. Use writing, cards or a preferred language, and seated rolling if needed. Harder: propose two solutions and explain each one's fairness.
## Timing and assessment
12 minutes: 2 explanation and agreement, 8 for four rounds, 2 reflection. Output: one factual sentence and selected restart rule. Record listening, shared speaking and return to activity, not students' character or obedience.
