## Setup and goal
Ages 8–13; pair in a 2×8 m lane, three soft collapsible obstacles up to 10 cm high, 1.5 m apart. Goal: lift foot enough to clear while walking under control.
## Procedure and timing
6 minutes: 1 demonstration, 4 practice and rest, 1 feedback and collection. Walk up, move one foot over then the other without jumping. Pass all three, stop and return outside. One performer at a time; observer outside lane. Change leading foot next pass; up to three passes each.
## Coaching and safety
No jumping, pushing or racing. Obstacles are not fixed or rigid sticks. Contact: stop and reset only with empty lane. Excessive trunk lean: lower or remove obstacle. No partner pulling a hand for assistance; stop for pain or instability.
## Adaptations and assessment
Easier: flat floor strips. Harder: continuous sequence without pauses, walking only and no height increase. For mobility aids replace obstacles with flat wide passage markers; accessible route, not forced obstacle crossing. Observe three controlled crossings with both feet clearing.
