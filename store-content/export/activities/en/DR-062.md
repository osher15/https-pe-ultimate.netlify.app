## Setup and goal
Ages 12–17; pair, ball and two markers in a 3×8 m lane. Ball holder stands 5 m from the start; stop line is 2 m before them. Goal: establish a defensive stance without invading their space.
## Procedure
Jog forward, shorten steps before the line and stop behind it, raising one hand comfortably. Ball holder stays still. Turn and walk back. Swap after 4 attempts; up to 2 rounds, resting during the partner's turn.
## Safety and adaptations
No jumping, stealing or movement by the ball holder. Walk if braking is uncontrolled. Harder: after a stable stop, name the side the holder points to. Wheelchair option: approach and stop in an adapted space, without requiring a raised hand while propelling.
## Timing and assessment
Success: stop before the line, stable base and eyes on the ball in 3 of 4 attempts; speed is not scored.
Suggested duration: 6 minutes — 1 for demonstration, 4 for turns, retrieval and rests, 1 for feedback. Start after appropriate physical preparation; stop for pain or dizziness.
