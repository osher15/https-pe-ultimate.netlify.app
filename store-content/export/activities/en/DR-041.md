## Setup and goal
Ages 10–15; suitable basketball and 3×3 m square each. Goal: transfer dribble between hands in front without gathering.
## Procedure and timing
6 minutes: 1 demonstration, 4 practice and rest, 1 feedback. Two right-hand bounces, diagonal bounce across front to left, two left-hand bounces, then reverse. Four transfers, then 20 seconds rest.
## Coaching and safety
Comfortable knees, ball below waist, hand above or beside ball without carrying underneath. Escaping ball: slow and narrow transfer. No defender or chasing outside square; stop before retrieval.
## Adaptations and assessment
Easier: one transfer then stop. Harder: briefly look at teacher's signal without accelerating. Stable sitting with leg clearance is possible. Observe four controlled transfers; focus is hand change, not pace change.
