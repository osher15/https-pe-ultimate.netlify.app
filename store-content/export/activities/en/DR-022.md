## Setup and goal
Ages 11–15; three pupils in a 4×6 m area. Start marker and two 1 m exit gates diagonally ahead, 2 m from start. Goal: suggest one direction with the body and exit under control in the other. No defender.
## Procedure and timing
6 minutes: 1 demonstration, 4 practice and swaps, 1 feedback. Shift weight slightly with a short step to one side, return through centre and take two steps towards the opposite gate. Slow and stop beyond it. No sharp pivot on a fixed foot. Walk back around; four attempts, two each side. After four attempts, the performer becomes the observer, the observer moves to the waiting position behind the start, and the waiting pupil behind the start takes the next turn as performer. Continue in this order so all three rotate through each role.
## Coaching and safety
Small clear feint, eyes forward and comfortable push-off. Lost balance: smaller step, slower pace. Leave 2 m stopping space beyond gates. No stealing, blocking, contact or speed contest; one performer at a time. Stop for knee or ankle pain.
## Adaptations and assessment
Easier: pause between feint and exit. Harder: observer points to the open gate before starting; feint away from it then exit through it, without time pressure. Mobility-aid users may look one way then make a broad turn the other as an adapted goal. Observe four attempts for clear direction change and controlled stopping.
