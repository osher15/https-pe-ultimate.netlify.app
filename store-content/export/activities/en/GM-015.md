## Setup and goal
Ages 9–16; 6–8 participants, intact strong 3×3 m fabric sheet and light foam ball. Four flat fabric markers securely attached, one near each side, half a metre from the edge. Clear 6×6 m area. Goal: guide the ball together without dropping it.
## Procedure, rules and ending
Hold the edge at a comfortable height no higher than the waist. Teacher places the ball centrally and names a marker. Gently tilt until the ball touches it; try to keep it there for 3 seconds. Success earns a point. Each task lasts 30 seconds including reset: lower together and teacher replaces ball centrally. A fall or time expiry earns no point; move to next task. Two rounds of 4 tasks, with 30 seconds rest between; participants take turns suggesting direction.
## Safety and adaptations
No shaking, bouncing, wrapping hands in fabric or going underneath. Retrieve fallen ball only with sheet lowered and everyone stopped. Easier: larger marker or no 3-second hold, scored separately. Harder: fewer teacher prompts. Stable seated or wheelchair access requires a comfortable common holding height.
## Timing and assessment
8 minutes: 1 explanation, 1 trial, 2 round, 0.5 rest, 2 round, 1.5 feedback and collection. Up to 4 points per round; compare the team's coordination with itself, not participants' strength.
