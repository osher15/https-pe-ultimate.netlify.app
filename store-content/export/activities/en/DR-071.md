## Setup and goal
Ages 11–16; pair, light foam ball and 5×10 m lane, target 3 m away. Goal: continuous pushing action as preparation for putting a ball, without heavy weight.
## Procedure
Hold the ball in the palm beside the shoulder, elbow comfortable. From a staggered stance, push forwards and upwards by extending the arm; no backward swing. Take 3 attempts per hand, retrieving after each, then swap; up to 2 rounds.
## Safety and adaptations
Observer behind the line; landing area empty. No metal shot or weight. Easier: smaller ball and shorter range. Harder: broad marked target. A stable seated version avoids forced trunk rotation.
## Timing and assessment
Target: 2 of 3 pushes per side without dropping the ball into a throwing backswing. Assess movement, not distance; rest while observing.
Suggested duration: 6 minutes — 1 for demonstration, 4 for turns, retrieval and rests, 1 for feedback. Start after appropriate physical preparation; stop for pain or dizziness.
