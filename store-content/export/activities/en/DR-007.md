## Identity and setup
Ages 8–13; independent standing or adapted stable support. Individuals in a 2×2 m square; 3 flat markers each, one for standing and two close in front. Targets are within comfortable reach, not maximal range. For 30: 90 markers.
## Observable goal
Touch a marker with the free foot and return under control in 4 of 5 attempts per side, using the agreed support.
## Procedure and timing
5 minutes: 1 demonstration, 3 practice, 1 checking. Shift weight onto one leg, lightly touch a front marker with the other foot and return. Light floor contact is allowed. Change sides after five repetitions. Stand on both feet and rest as needed between rounds.
## Coaching and error correction
Look forward, keep the supporting knee soft and the reach short. If the body falls sideways, bring the marker closer or add support. If contact is heavy, slow down and shorten the reach.
## Safety
No closed eyes, raised surfaces or external pushes. Use only an approved stable wall or rail for support, not a loose chair or pulling on a partner. Keep each pupil's area clear.
## Adaptations and assessment
Easier: continuous light foot contact and hand support. Harder: a short pause before returning, without moving the target farther. From stable sitting, a small weight shift and hand reach can serve a different goal. Count a controlled return without a sudden grab; record support used and do not compare unlike adaptations.
