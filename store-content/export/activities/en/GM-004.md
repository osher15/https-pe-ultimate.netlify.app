## Setup and goal
Ages 11–15; controlled passing and catching. Four in a 5×5 m square, soft ball and four boundary markers. Three passers near the edge and one central interceptor. Goal: change angles and choose an open receiver.
## Timing and rules
9 minutes: 2 explanation, 3 play, 1 rest and feedback, 2 play, 1 review. Without the ball walk along the boundary; carrier stands up to 4 seconds. Five consecutive passes earn one point for the whole four and reset the count. Airborne interception, drop, out-of-bounds or time violation reset without punishment; a passer restarts at the boundary. Lines count in. Rotate interceptor every 30 seconds in fixed order regardless of interceptions. No elimination or individual points; review shared score at the end.
## Coaching and safety
Look before passing; teammates open an angle. Replace hard overhead throws with chest or bounce passes. Interceptor stays an arm's length from carrier and intercepts only after release. No contact, body blocking, hand steals or jumping into others. Stop everyone before retrieving out of bounds.
## Adaptations and assessment
Easier: walking interceptor and six seconds to pass. Harder: receiver changes angle before catching, without speeding up. Rolling passes and accessible positions may be agreed beforehand. Observe passing lanes, involvement and role rotation; score is not an individual measure.
