## Setup and goal
Ages 8–13; four players, 8 firm cards showing four pairs of teacher-drawn geometric symbols. 5×8 m area; cards face down in two rows on a stable accessible surface, 5 m from waiting base. Shared goal: find four pairs using memory and shared information.
## Procedure and timing
10 minutes: 2 explanation, 3 play, 1 strategy discussion, 3 replay, 1 review and collection. One walker per turn reveals two cards and shows group. Match: carry pair to base. No match: replace face down in same locations. Return before next player leaves. Fixed order, no bonus turn for success. After four pairs, reset together; teacher shuffles while group stays at base. Restart with a different first player.
## Coaching and safety
No running, pushing, throwing cards or two players at display together. Never put slippery cards on walking route. Discuss at base; no changing card locations mid-round. No penalty for errors or elimination.
## Adaptations and assessment
Easier: three pairs and large high-contrast symbols. Harder: six pairs if display surface fits. Accessible route, reachable cards and tactile symbols are possible; everyone chooses and reveals in turn. Assess accurate sharing and equal turns, not running time. This is a moving memory game, not vigorous fitness work.
