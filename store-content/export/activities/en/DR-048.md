## Setup and goal
Ages 11–16; trio, soft ball, 3×9 m lane. Three comfortably spaced step markers; feeder to side, final receiver 3 m beyond last marker. Goal: controlled catch–steps–pass sequence.
## Procedure and timing
7 minutes: 2 demonstration, 4 practice, 1 feedback. Catch standing before markers, walk three steps holding ball and stop. Pass to ready receiver; return ball before rotating. Aim for three turns each as time permits. Teaching sequence, not a test of all game rules.
## Coaching and safety
Gentle passes, walking only. Teacher adjusts markers instead of forcing stride length. No release to unready receiver or crossing active lane. Failed catch: stop and reset.
## Adaptations and assessment
Easier: catch and one step. Harder: pass immediately after third step without prolonged pause or jump. Accessible route may use three stopping points as a different goal. Observe sequence, ball control and looking before passing.
