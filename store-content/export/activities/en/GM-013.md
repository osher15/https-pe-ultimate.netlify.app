## Setup and goal
Ages 10–16; pair and bouncy foam ball. Two 3×3 m halves separated by a metre-wide neutral strip; a clear metre margin around the court. Goal: cooperative returns after a bounce.
## Procedure, rules and ending
Starter drops the ball to bounce in their own half and strikes with an open palm below waist height into the partner's half. Receiver allows one bounce there and returns. Each legal return adds 1 to the shared count; at 6, catch and start a new rally with the other starter. Two bounces, landing in the strip or outside the half, catching or double contact ends the rally. No penalty points: retrieve and restart when both are ready. Outer boundary lines belong to the half; strip lines belong to the strip.
## Safety and adaptations
Stay in your half; no diving, hard hits or chasing outside. Catch excessively high balls and reset. Easier: agree on two permitted bounces in advance; record separately. Accessible option: catch after a bounce and toss from stable seating, assessed separately. Harder: direct towards a broad area without extra force.
## Timing and assessment
8 minutes: 1 explanation, 1 trial, 5 play and retrieval, 1 feedback. Rest as needed between rallies. Record best rally and rallies involving success for both; no individual winner.
