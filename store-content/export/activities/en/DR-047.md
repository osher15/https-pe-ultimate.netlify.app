## Setup and goal
Ages 11–16; pair, soft ball securely held in one hand, 3×6 m lane. Distance 3 m. Goal: opposite-foot step and directed one-hand release.
## Procedure and timing
6 minutes: 1 demonstration, 4 practice and rest, 1 feedback. Ball near shoulder, elbow at comfortable height. When receiver shows ready hands, take a small opposite-foot step and release towards hands in front of chest. Four passes each then rest; change hand if comfortable.
## Coaching and safety
No forced high elbow or pulling arm behind body. Heavy or slippery ball: change equipment, do not grip harder. No face passes or steals. Both stop before retrieval.
## Adaptations and assessment
Easier: 2 m without step. Harder: comfortable side hand target without more force. Stable sitting with adapted distance is possible. Observe four passes for opposite-step coordination, release and control; do not compare hand strength.
