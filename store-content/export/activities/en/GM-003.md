## Setup and goal
Ages 8–12; four players, two on each side of a 4×8 m court. One soft ball and a secured sports net at adapted height. Without a safe net, use a wide floor strip, not a cord. Goal: build a shared rally involving all four. These are adapted game rules, not official volleyball.
## Timing and rules
10 minutes: 2 explanation, 3 play, 1 rest and feedback, 3 play, 1 review. Toss underarm with two hands across. Catch after at most one bounce. Carrier stands for up to 5 seconds, passes to the partner, who tosses across. A cross-net catch adds one shared rally point. Alternate first receiver each time the ball returns to that side. A second bounce, out-of-bounds, net contact or holding the ball for more than 5 seconds ends the rally; restart from the side where play stopped. Lines count in. No elimination; improve the previous rally.
## Coaching and safety
Signal or call before catching; partner clears space. Reduce force for long throws; designate a receiver if both approach. No spikes, blocks over the net, dives or travel underneath. Protect posts and clear their surroundings. Walk to retrieve out-of-bounds balls only after stopping.
## Adaptations and assessment
Easier: replace the net with a clearly marked wide floor strip separating the sides and allow two bounces. The ball must cross the strip; players stay on their own side during play. Harder: no bounce, without speeding up. Stable seated play uses a smaller court and reachable throws for all. Observe communication, rotation and controlled catches over three exchanges, not only points.
