## Setup and goal
Ages 8–13; soft ball small enough for secure two-handed handling, 2×2 m per pupil. Goal: hand transfers around body without throwing or large trunk rotation.
## Procedure and timing
5 minutes: 1 demonstration, 3 practice and rest, 1 feedback and collection. Start in front of abdomen. Transfer to one hand, guide to side and behind, hand to other hand and return in front. Maintain hold, no bounce. Up to three circles one way, stop, three opposite way. Rest 20 seconds before repeating if comfortable.
## Coaching and safety
Behind-back handover only within comfort; no shoulder pulling or forced reach. Dropped ball: stop and collect, no sudden bending during circling. No maximum speed, closed eyes or heavy ball. Stop for pain.
## Adaptations and assessment
Easier: front-to-side transfer only, no behind-back reach. Harder: change direction on cue after completing circle, without speed increase. Stable sitting if backrest permits; otherwise use front version. Observe control and clear handover, not competitive circle counts.
