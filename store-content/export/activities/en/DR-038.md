## Setup and goal
Ages 9–14; pair in a 2×10 m lane, 6 m movement plus 4 m slowing space. No equipment. Goal: step then small hop on same foot, followed by opposite side.
## Procedure and timing
6 minutes: 2 demonstration and breakdown, 3 practice and rest, 1 feedback. Step right, small hop right; step left, small hop left. Continue to 6 m line, transition to walking and stop. Return outside lane before partner starts. Up to three passes each if comfortable.
## Coaching and safety
Low height, even rhythm, not distance or speed. Repeated hops on one foot: stop, practise one step-hop each side. Dry clear surface, no race or chase. Stop for pain or uncontrolled landing; no stairs.
## Adaptations and assessment
Easier: step and heel lift instead of hop as preparation. Harder: coordinate opposite arm and leg without changing speed. If hopping is unsuitable, seated knee-lift sequence is a different rhythm goal. Observe four step-hop cycles and side changes, not travel time.
