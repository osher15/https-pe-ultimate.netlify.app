## Setup and goal
Ages 8–14; 8–16 participants, an even number, working in pairs. Each pair has a 2×6 m lane with a metre gap from adjacent lanes; observer stands outside. Two large cards: circle and triangle. Goal: select the correct response while controlling movement.
## Procedure, rules and ending
Circle means walk in the lane; triangle means stop steadily. At the end, turn while walking without leaving the lane. Teacher displays and names a symbol at seconds 0, 10, 20, 30, 40 and 50, varying the order of the symbols. Each instruction applies until the next signal or the emergency stop; the round ends at second 60. Observer awards a point for the correct response within 3 seconds, maintained until the next signal. Swap after the minute. An error never eliminates anyone: correct and continue at the next signal. Fixed emergency signal — raised palm and 'stop' — always overrides cards.
## Safety and adaptations
No running or deliberate collision. Allow room to stop and turn. Easier: earlier cue. Harder in a future round: reverse shape meanings only after stopping and explaining. Emergency signal never changes. Use a suitably wide wheelchair lane or seated arm movement versus stillness.
## Timing and assessment
7 minutes: 1 explanation, 1 trial, 1 round, 1 swap and rest, 1 round, 2 feedback and closing. Up to 6 points each for private feedback only. Also check stable stopping without loss of balance.
