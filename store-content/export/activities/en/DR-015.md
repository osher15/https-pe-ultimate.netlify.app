## Setup and goal
Ages 8–13. One pair per clear 2×4 m lane; one short, light racket and foam ball per pair. One performs; the observer stays outside racket reach. For 30 pupils: 15 rackets and 15 balls, only if 15 separate lanes fit. Goal: four controlled steps with the ball remaining on the racket, without bouncing or touching it with the other hand.
## Procedure
6 minutes: 1 demonstration, 4 practice, 1 feedback and collection. Place the ball on the racket by hand, settle it, walk four ordinary steps and stop. Remove the ball by hand and exchange roles. If it falls, stop, put the racket somewhere safe and retrieve only when the lane is clear.
## Coaching and safety
Comfortable grip; racket in front at a comfortable height with a level face. Ball rolls forward: level the face before the next step. Hand fatigue: shorter turns or a lighter racket. No hitting, overhead swings, chasing or speed contests. One performer per lane; return to the start only after stopping.
## Adaptations and assessment
Easier: balance stationary for 5 seconds or support the handle with the other hand. Harder: pause midway through the four steps, without bouncing. Use stable seated balance or an accessible movement route; do not require simultaneous wheelchair propulsion and racket carrying if unsafe. Record control across three attempts at the chosen level, not speed.
