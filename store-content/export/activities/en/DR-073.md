## Setup and goal
Ages 11–16; pair and 3×10 m lane on a suitable sports surface. Flat take-off mark and clear 3 m landing zone. Goal: connect a short approach to take-off and landing; stable standing-jump landing required.
## Procedure
Take 3 walking steps, push from the final foot into a small forward jump and land on both feet. Hold 2 seconds and exit sideways. Return outside the lane; 3 attempts then swap, up to 2 rounds.
## Safety and adaptations
Only one performer in the lane; observer behind the start. No board, obstacle or maximum-distance attempt. Easier: slow approach and tiny jump. If jumping is unsuitable, practise a step and stop, assessed separately. Harder: smoother sequence at the same distance.
## Timing and assessment
Target: 2 of 3 controlled sequences with one-foot take-off and two-foot landing. Rest during partner's turn; exact contact with the mark is not required.
Suggested duration: 6 minutes — 1 for demonstration, 4 for turns, retrieval and rests, 1 for feedback. Start after appropriate physical preparation; stop for pain or dizziness.
