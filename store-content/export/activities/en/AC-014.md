## Setup and goal
Ages 11–17; 8 participants in four pairs, two foam balls and fixture sheet. Two 3×8 m lanes separated by 2 m; each has a flat metre-wide gate 3 m from the start. Goal: build a turn schedule without a pair waiting more than one round.
## Procedure, rules and ending
Label pairs A–D. Two pairs per lane alternate rolling through the gate; partners alternate within each pair. Full passage earns a point; contact with a gate marker or a miss earns none. Retrieve after the ball stops; others wait behind the line. Fixtures: first A–B and C–D; second A–C and B–D; third A–D and B–C. Each round is 2 minutes; after 90 seconds do not start a new pair of turns; finish by the end of the second minute. An unfinished pair of turns is excluded from scoring; record any resulting opportunity gap. Change the starting member within each pair each round. Scores never decide who continues: everyone plays every round.
## Safety and adaptations
Never cross lanes during rolling. If only one lane is available, do not crowd: run one fixture at a time and reduce event scope, not safety gaps; record that the full schedule was not completed. Ramp or seated rolling can use an agreed fixed line. No elimination or exercise punishment.
## Timing and assessment
12 minutes: 2 schedule design, 6 for three rounds, 2 transitions and retrieval between rounds, 2 reflection. Output: fixtures and time/opportunities per pair. Check feasibility; no champion required. Ties stand.
