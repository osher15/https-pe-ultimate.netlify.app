## Setup and goal
Ages 10–16; 4 participants, soft football and 6 soft cones. In a 14×14 m area, mark 3 gates 1.5 m wide, at least 3 m apart and from boundaries. Each gate has a different symbol. Goal: pass through every gate and involve everyone.
## Procedure, rules and ending
Team chooses the next gate. Walking dribbles and ground passes are allowed. Extra passes that do not go through a gate are allowed so all four players can receive; these passes do not collect a gate. Collect a gate when a pass travels between its cones and a teammate stops the ball beyond it; cone contact does not count. Each gate counts once per sequence. Complete the task only after all 3 gates and a received pass for each of the four players. Stop and begin another sequence. Retrieve an out-of-bounds ball walking and restart from the boundary; collected gates remain. No opponent or steals.
## Safety and adaptations
Do not wait between cones; receiver stands beyond the gate and in view. No high kicks. Stop and coordinate if paths cross. Easier: wider gates. Harder: a different receiver at each gate. Hand rolling or an adapted ramp can preserve route choice and everyone's involvement.
## Timing and assessment
9 minutes: 1 explanation, 1 planning, 6 play and rests, 1 discussion. Take turns leading route choice. Record completed tasks and involvement in receiving, without speed rankings.
