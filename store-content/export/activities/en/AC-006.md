## Setup and goal
Ages 9–14; four pupils in an 8×8 m area. Four cones form a 4 m square inside it, each with a teacher-drawn circle, triangle, square or star. Cards fixed to cones, not on walking paths. Waiting base at side. Teacher draws matching map showing base and an arrow towards a particular wall. Goal: align map with space and visit three points in order.
## Procedure and timing
12 minutes: 2 explanation, 2 map orientation, 6 turns, 2 review and collection. Teacher provides four different three-symbol sequences. Each turn one navigator walks alone following their sequence, stops one step from each cone and names symbol without touching, then returns to base. Others check order, hold sequence card and check clear path. Rotate every 90 seconds so all four navigate. Map arrow refers to designated wall, not necessarily north.
## Safety and management
Eyes open, walking only. No racing, collecting objects or searching outside area; stop to read rather than hold map in front of face while moving. If blocked, stop and ask to clear path. Waiting pupils remain at base without crossing. End turn even if sequence unfinished; no punishment.
## Adaptations and assessment
Easier: two points and map aligned with area. Harder: rotate and realign map before starting, without changing speed. Use large symbols, spoken sequence, tactile map before starting and mobility-aid spacing; no blindfold navigation. Record order, use of reference point and correction after stopping.
