## Setup and goal
Ages 8–13; soft bouncy ball, 2×2 m each. Goal: time a brief action during bounce and catch with both hands.
## Procedure and timing
6 minutes: 1 demonstration, 4 practice and rest, 1 feedback and collection. Push ball down with both hands from waist height using moderate force. Clap once, immediately prepare hands, catch after one floor bounce. Five attempts then 20 seconds rest. Reset stable stance each time.
## Coaching and safety
No hard slams for extra time, behind-back claps or overhead catches. High rebound: reduce force. Escaping ball: stop and retrieve walking. No clap-count contest.
## Adaptations and assessment
Easier: bounce and catch without clap as preparation. Harder: clap only after floor contact without adding claps. Stable sitting with comfortable bounce height possible. Observe four of five catches and height control; easier attempts are not full-version completions.
