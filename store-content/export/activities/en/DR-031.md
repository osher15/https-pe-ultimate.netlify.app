## Setup and goal
Ages 9–14; sound appropriately sized rope and clear 3×3 m square per performer. Comfortable small jumping and landing are prerequisites; continuous skipping is unnecessary. In pairs, observer stays outside square.
## Procedure and timing
6 minutes: 2 demonstration and preparation, 3 practice and rest, 1 feedback and collection. Rope starts behind feet. Turn forward; as it approaches floor in front, jump slightly with both feet so rope passes beneath. Land softly and stop. Up to five separate attempts, at least 30 seconds rest, then swap. Goal: coordinate turn and jump, not height.
## Coaching and safety
Comfortably close elbows, controlled turn and soft knees on landing. Tangled rope: stop, put it down and reset, never pull hard. No high jumps, tying rope to body or swinging near others. Dry non-slip surface; stop for pain or fatigue affecting landing.
## Adaptations and assessment
Easier: turn, stop, then step over—preparation, not a successful jump. Harder: only two consecutive jumps, then stop. If jumping is unsuitable, seated heel raises with arm timing and no rope offer a different goal. Record attempts with rope clearance and controlled landing; no maximum-streak ranking.
