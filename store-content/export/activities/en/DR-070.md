## Setup and goal
Ages 10–16; pair, soft flying disc and 5×12 m lane. Broad flat target 4 m away. Goal: release the disc nearly level into empty space.
## Procedure
Grip with thumb on top and fingers under the rim. Move the arm from beside the body forwards, extend the elbow comfortably and release with a short wrist action. Retrieve after it stops. Take 4 throws and swap with the observer behind the line; up to 2 rounds.
## Safety and adaptations
No receiver, body targets or high throws in strong wind. Easier: closer target. Harder: narrower landing area without extra force. Throwing from a stable seat is possible with arm-side clearance.
## Timing and assessment
Look for a nearly level release and forward flight in 3 of 4 throws; record target accuracy separately. Rest while observing.
Suggested duration: 6 minutes — 1 for demonstration, 4 for turns, retrieval and rests, 1 for feedback. Start after appropriate physical preparation; stop for pain or dizziness.
