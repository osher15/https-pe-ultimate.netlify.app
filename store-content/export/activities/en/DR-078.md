## Setup and goal
Ages 11–17; pair, mat and 2×3 m space. Goal: move the leg sideways without rolling backwards.
## Procedure
Lie on the side with head supported on the arm, lower leg comfortably bent and upper leg nearly straight in line with the body. Lift the upper leg slightly and lower slowly. Take 4 repetitions, change sides, then swap; up to 2 rounds.
## Safety and adaptations
No required height, weights or partner pulling. Toes face forwards relative to the body, not the ceiling. Easier: smaller range. Harder: lower for 3 seconds. Standing alternative: small side leg lift with wall support and upright trunk.
## Timing and assessment
Observer checks a steady pelvis in 3 of 4 repetitions per side. Rest at least 30 seconds before repeating that side; observer gives no hands-on correction.
Suggested duration: 6 minutes — 1 for demonstration, 4 for turns, retrieval and rests, 1 for feedback. Start after appropriate physical preparation; stop for pain or dizziness.
