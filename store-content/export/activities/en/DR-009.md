## Setup and goal
Ages 10–14; one light soft ball and a 3×3 m square with overhead clearance per pupil. For 30: 30 balls; with 15, use pairs and swap performer–observer every 30 seconds. Goal: make a low overhead set to self and catch within personal space in 4 of 5 attempts.
## Procedure
6 minutes: 1 demonstration, 4 practice, 1 feedback and collection. Toss just above the forehead, meet with comfortably spread fingers and gently push upward. Catch after one contact and settle before restarting. No continuous sequence or high set required.
## Coaching and safety
Hands above forehead, elbows slightly bent. Ball falling forward: lower toss and prepare hands earlier. Sideways ball: balance the hands. No locked fingers or forced positions. Stay clear of baskets, lights and fans; no jumping or chasing into another area. Finger pain means stop and adapt.
## Adaptations and assessment
Easier: toss and catch above forehead without a set—a preparation stage, not the same test. Harder: two low sets then catch. Stable seated practice with a reachable toss is possible. Observe five attempts at the selected level; record control and hand position, not speed.
