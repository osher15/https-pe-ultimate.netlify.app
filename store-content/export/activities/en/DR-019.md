## Setup and goal
Ages 9–14; pairs in a 2×5 m lane, two markers 3 m apart. No ball or opponent. Goal: move sideways in both directions without crossing feet or losing balance.
## Procedure and timing
5 minutes: 1 demonstration, 3 practice and rest, 1 feedback. Keep facing the same way. The foot nearest the travel direction moves first; the other follows without touching or crossing it. Travel 3 m, stop, return sideways. Two out-and-back trips then swap; observer outside lane.
## Coaching and safety
Slight comfortable knee bend, short steps and steady head. Crossing: slow down and separate step–follow actions. No deep squat, jumping, sprinting, pushing or reaching to touch markers. Dry surface; stop for pain or stumbling. No performers moving towards each other.
## Adaptations and assessment
Easier: 1 m at walking pace. Harder: choose direction from a visual cue only after stopping, without increasing speed. For mobility-aid users, adapt turning and stopping as a different goal; do not demand unavailable sideways movement. Observe two passes each way: foot order, spacing and stable stop.
