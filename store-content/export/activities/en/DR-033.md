## Setup and goal
Ages 10–15; soft football per pupil in a 3×3 m area. Goal: pull ball back with forefoot sole, turn using steps and exit in a new direction with ball close.
## Procedure and timing
6 minutes: 1 demonstration, 4 practice and rest, 1 feedback and collection. Lightly place front of sole on stationary ball ahead. Pull back a short distance, return foot to ground, turn body a quarter turn using small steps. Take one gentle touch into new direction and stop. Three attempts per foot, fully resetting between attempts.
## Coaching and safety
Weight remains mainly on support leg during pull; never stand on ball. Do not twist knee above a fixed foot. Escaping ball: shorter pull; lost balance: practise pull only. No opponent, tackling or entering another space. Stop for pain.
## Adaptations and assessment
Easier: short pull and stop without turn as preparation. Harder: smoothly connect pull and turn without more speed. Light hand support on a fixed rail may be teacher-approved with clear turning space; do not require this version from someone unable to stand. Record ball control and direction change using steps.
