## Setup and goal
Ages 8–13; pair, light intact hoop, 2×8 m lane with control line at 4 m and another 4 m clear. Goal: roll a nearly vertical hoop straight without running after it.
## Procedure and timing
6 minutes: 1 demonstration, 4 practice and swaps, 1 feedback and collection. Stand at start, hold upright hoop by rim and push gently forward. Stay behind start line. Once hoop stops or lies down, walk to collect and carry it back. Three attempts then swap; observer beside and behind start.
## Coaching and safety
No backspin to make it return, putting people through hoop or catching it in motion. Sideways curve: release more upright and reduce force. Beyond 4 m line: reduce force next attempt. If it enters another lane, stop and request clearance before retrieval.
## Adaptations and assessment
Easier: 2 m distance goal. Harder: pass between two markers 1 m apart at 3 m, without more speed. Stable seated release at accessible height with retrieval assistance is possible. Assess direction and controlled release, not maximum distance.
