## Setup and goal
Ages 10–17; 4 participants, light ball and secured hoop at adapted height. Clear 6×6 m area. Shooting marks 1.5 and 2.5 m from the point directly beneath the rim centre. Goal: choose distance based on likelihood and points.
## Procedure, rules and ending
Before the turn choose: near basket earns one point, far basket 2. One attempt per player per round; a miss earns none with no retry. Others retrieve, record and encourage; rotate after every shot. Retriever waits to the side outside the ball path and collects only after the ball lands and shooter stops. Play 3 rounds of 4 shots, up to 8 team points per round. Shooter chooses without team pressure.
## Safety and adaptations
No defender, chasing rebounds or jumping towards the hoop. Others stay outside shooting space. Check portable hoop anchoring. Stable seated shooting and two agreed personal distances are possible, recorded beforehand. Easier: lower hoop and smaller ball. Harder: explain choice before the turn, not more force.
## Timing and assessment
10 minutes: 2 explanation/adaptations, 6 play/collection, 2 reflection. End after a full round if time is short without rushing collection. Record decisions and experience-based changes; score is not an individual ability test.
