## Setup and goal
Ages 12–16; pair in a 2×5 m lane, no ball or net. Two floor markers at comfortable teacher-adjusted step distances. Goal: learn a step sequence ending in stable stance; no actual volleyball spike.
## Procedure and timing
6 minutes: 2 demonstration, 3 practice and rest, 1 feedback. Right-handed hitter: right step then left, second foot joining in a comfortable slightly forward position; reverse for left-handed hitter. Walk the sequence, stop for two seconds, return around outside. Four attempts then swap. Observer outside lane.
## Coaching and safety
No forcing feet together, aggressive braking, jumping or hard arm swing. Crossing feet: slow down and reset markers. Leave at least 2 m beyond second marker. Stop for knee or ankle pain.
## Adaptations and assessment
Easier: separate steps with pauses. Harder: slightly smoother sequence, still walking. If stepping is inaccessible, seated arm sequence is a different rhythm goal. Observe foot order and final stability; preparation only, not a jump assessment.
