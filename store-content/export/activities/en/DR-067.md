## Setup and goal
Ages 12–17; pair and flat centre marker in a 3×3 m area. Goal: rise vertically with both hands and return to the take-off area. Controlled landing required.
## Procedure
From a comfortable stance, bend knees slightly, jump low and raise both hands overhead without a backward arm swing. Land on both feet and pause 3 seconds. Take 4 repetitions, then swap with the observer outside; up to 2 rounds.
## Safety and adaptations
No net, ball, nearby wall or jump towards anyone. Reduce height if drifting forwards. Easier: rise onto the forefeet. Seated adaptation: reach both hands up with stable trunk; this does not assess jumping. Harder: return more precisely to the same place.
## Timing and assessment
Target: 3 of 4 landings within the take-off area without a large recovery step. Rest throughout the partner's turn.
Suggested duration: 6 minutes — 1 for demonstration, 4 for turns, retrieval and rests, 1 for feedback. Start after appropriate physical preparation; stop for pain or dizziness.
