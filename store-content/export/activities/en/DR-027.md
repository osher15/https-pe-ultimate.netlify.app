## Setup and goal
Ages 10–15; pair, short badminton racket and intact shuttle in a 3×3 m area with clear overhead space. Goal: gentle contact beneath shuttle, lifting it to comfortable catching height.
## Procedure and timing
6 minutes: 1 demonstration, 4 practice and swaps, 1 feedback and collection. Hold shuttle in free hand ahead of body, release just above racket and tap upward once with a short action. Once racket stops, catch with free hand. Five attempts then swap; observer outside racket reach.
## Coaching and safety
Start racket below shuttle; no overhead swing or hard hit. Sideways flight: level face slightly and shorten action. No catching beside a moving racket or chasing into another area. Stop before floor retrieval.
## Adaptations and assessment
Easier: larger slow shuttle and shorter drop. Harder: two gentle taps before catch. Stable sitting and close reach are possible. In three attempts observe contact underneath, controlled height and separation of strike and catch; no maximum-height target.
