## Setup and goal
Ages 10–15; pair and 2×6 m lane with 3 m clear for travel. Goal: sustain a short single-foot sequence. Comfortable single-leg standing required.
## Procedure
Make 3 small forward hops on the same foot, place the other foot down and stop. Walk back outside the lane and rest 20 seconds. Try the other leg, then swap; up to 2 rounds.
## Safety and adaptations
Observer outside the lane; no obstacles or chase. End the sequence if control is lost. Easier: one hop then both feet down. Harder: even rhythm over the same 3 hops without more distance. Non-jumping alternative: supported weight transfer, assessed separately.
## Timing and assessment
Record whether 3 controlled hops were completed on each side, without comparing students or demanding perfect symmetry.
Suggested duration: 6 minutes — 1 for demonstration, 4 for turns, retrieval and rests, 1 for feedback. Start after appropriate physical preparation; stop for pain or dizziness.
