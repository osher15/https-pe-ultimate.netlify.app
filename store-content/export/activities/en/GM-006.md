## Setup and goal
Ages 9–14; four players in a 3×8 m lane. Six soft beanbags at base and three flat targets at far end, 5 m away and 1 m apart. Shared goal: finish with two bags on each target, without racing another group.
## Rules and timing
10 minutes: 2 explanation and planning, 3 play, 1 rest and discussion, 3 replay, 1 review. Fixed order, one walker at a time carries one bag by hand to a chosen target. Return through lane and verbally signal next player waiting at base. If a target has more than two, the next turn may move one bag between targets instead of taking from base. When each has two, finish and check together; restart only with approval after resetting bags. Change starting player in second round.
## Safety and management
No throwing bags, contact handovers, overtaking or two departures together. Three waiting players behind base line; no collecting in other lanes. Targets large enough to place without stepping on them. Stop if dropped bag obstructs movement. No speed scoring or exercise punishment.
## Adaptations and assessment
Easier: two targets and four bags. Harder: same six bags with agreed distribution of 1, 2 and 3. Targets may sit on stable accessible-height surfaces with adapted carrying, approved by teacher beforehand. Everyone carries in turn accessibly. Observe planning, error correction and turn sharing; distribution changes are versions of one game.
