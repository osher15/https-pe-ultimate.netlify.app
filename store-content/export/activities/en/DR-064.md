## Setup and goal
Ages 10–16; pair, foam ball and 3×3 m area. Goal: slow the ball using the front of the thigh rather than the knee.
## Procedure
Toss slightly above waist height, lift the thigh comfortably and lower it slightly on contact. Let the ball fall and collect by hand once still. Take 4 attempts per leg, then swap with the observer outside; up to 2 rounds.
## Safety and adaptations
No repeated juggling, heading or forced leg lift. Do not chase a ball outside the area. Easier: release close above the thigh. Harder: make the ball drop closer. From a stable seat, cushion on the thigh only if it can move comfortably.
## Timing and assessment
Look for thigh contact and controlled lowering in 3 of 4 attempts; rebound height is not an achievement. Rest during swaps and observation.
Suggested duration: 6 minutes — 1 for demonstration, 4 for turns, retrieval and rests, 1 for feedback. Start after appropriate physical preparation; stop for pain or dizziness.
