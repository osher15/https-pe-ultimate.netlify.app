## Setup and goal
Ages 11–15; smooth stable wall, 1 m width per pupil, no additional equipment. Stand facing away with heels about 15 cm from wall. Goal: move hips back with comfortable hip flexion and return under control.
## Procedure and timing
5 minutes: 1 demonstration, 3 practice and rest, 1 feedback. Feet comfortably apart, knees slightly bent. Send hips back to touch wall lightly; trunk inclines forward as a unit within comfortable range. Return upright without leaning backward. Four repetitions, at least 30 seconds rest; up to two rounds.
## Coaching and safety
No depth target or stretching into pain. If it becomes a low squat, reduce range and move slightly closer to wall. If back rounds with effort, reduce range; no forced straightening or partner handling. No weights, pushing into wall or breath holding. Stop for pain or dizziness.
## Adaptations and assessment
Easier: closer wall, smaller movement. Harder: comfortable two-second pause before returning, without added weight. Do not require this exercise if standing is uncomfortable; a small supported seated trunk movement is a different task subject to individual adaptation. Observe hip movement, breathing and steady return; do not score depth.
