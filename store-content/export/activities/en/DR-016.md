## Setup and goal
Ages 9–13. Three pupils per station: sound, secured basket, appropriately sized and weighted ball, and at least 5×5 m of clear space without crossing traffic. Teacher adjusts basket height if equipment permits. Shooting spot 1–2 m away. Goal: controlled release and balance in four of five opportunities, not only scoring.
## Procedure
7 minutes: 2 demonstration, 4 practice, 1 feedback. Roles: shooter, collector and observer. From a comfortable stance, direct the ball upward and forward with the shooting hand; the other hand supports the side. Take one shot without jumping and stay still. Collector waits to the side outside the ball path, retrieves only after it lands and when safe, then returns it. Rotate roles in a fixed order.
## Coaching and safety
Supporting hand does not push sideways. If effort reduces control, move closer or adapt ball and basket height. Lost balance: reset; do not force identical joint angles for everyone. No contested rebounds, jumping to retrieve, hanging on the rim or standing under a descending ball. Protect base and posts; stop if another ball enters.
## Adaptations and assessment
Easier: closer, lower target, which need not be a basket. Harder: move slightly farther only while retaining control. From stable sitting, adapt target and ball while preserving needed support. Record release, balance and actual attempts; with fewer than five, do not report a score out of five.
