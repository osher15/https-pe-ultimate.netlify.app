## Setup and goal
Ages 10–15; sound mat per pupil and 2×2 m space. For pupils comfortable lying on their back and getting up from the floor. Goal: slide one leg without rocking the pelvis while breathing continuously.
## Procedure and timing
5 minutes: 1 demonstration, 3 practice and rest, 1 feedback and slow rising. Lie on back, knees bent, feet on mat. Slide one heel a short distance forward without lifting, then return. Alternate legs; four each side, rest, up to two comfortable rounds. Full knee straightening is not required.
## Coaching and safety
Pelvis quiet, back comfortable; do not force the back against the floor. Pelvis rotates or back arches more: shorten slide. No lifting both legs, abdominal-hold contest or breath holding. Stop for back or hip pain. Mat stays secure; others do not pass around the head.
## Adaptations and assessment
Easier: very short slide. Harder: two-second pause midway within comfortable range, heel down. If lying is inaccessible, a stable seated heel slide on a sufficiently smooth floor offers a different goal; chair remains fixed. Observe four movements per side and continuous breathing; early rest is allowed.
