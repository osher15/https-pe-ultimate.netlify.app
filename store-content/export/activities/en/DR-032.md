## Setup and goal
Ages 10–15; pair in a 3×6 m channel, sound PE play stick with soft blade and foam ball. Feeder 3 m from receiver. Goal: stop a slow ball beside blade without hitting it back.
## Procedure and timing
6 minutes: 1 demonstration, 4 practice and swaps, 1 feedback and collection. Feeder rolls by hand only when receiver is ready. Receiver holds with both hands, places blade on floor in ball path and cushions contact with a short backward movement. Stop fully, put stick down safely and return ball by hand roll. Swap after five attempts.
## Coaching and safety
Watch ball, comfortable grip, blade on floor. Ball rebounds: reduce feed speed and steady blade. No swinging, raising stick above knees, tackling or foot stopping. Nobody else within stick reach; retrieve escaped ball only after stopping.
## Adaptations and assessment
Easier: feed from 2 m. Harder: slightly left or right within one-step reach, without speeding up. Stable sitting with adapted stick length and space requires teacher approval. Observe four of five stops close to blade; do not improvise with a rigid stick if suitable equipment is unavailable.
