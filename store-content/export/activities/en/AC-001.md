## Identity and setup
Ages 8–14. Groups of four in a 4×4 m square; one personal spot per pupil and 4 boundary markers per group. No external music, screens or other equipment. Teacher counts or claps a comfortable pulse.
## Observable goal
Compose and perform eight beats using at least two movement types, with an idea and role from every participant.
## Procedure and timing
12 minutes: 2 demonstration and explanation, 3 planning, 4 rehearsal, 2 sharing between paired groups, 1 reflection. Choose only marching in place, a small side-step, an arm reach or a gentle clap. Each pupil proposes two beats; combine in an agreed order. Rehearse for 30 seconds then rest/adjust for 30 seconds, four times. Each group shows twice; observers give one factual description. No competitive score.
## Coaching and error correction
Count 1–8 and restart together. If timing is lost, slow down and learn four beats at a time. If crowded, choose stationary movements and remove crossing paths.
## Safety
No lifts, rolls, high jumps, forced contact or closed eyes. Maintain safe reach space. Clapping can be omitted for sound sensitivity.
## Adaptations and assessment
Easier: repeat four beats twice. Harder: change arm direction in the second sequence without changing location. Seated movement offers an equally valid rhythm and creativity goal. Assess two eight-beat performances, two movement types and input from all four; do not assess appearance or dance background.
