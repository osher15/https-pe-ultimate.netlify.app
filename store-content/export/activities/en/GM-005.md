## Setup and goal
Ages 8–12; eight players in a clear 10×10 m area, four cones outside boundary. One tagger wears a loose armband with no trailing ends. Game goal: avoid a tag while walking and managing space; no points or elimination.
## Rules and timing
8 minutes: 2 explanation and demonstration, 2 play, 1 rest and feedback, 2 play, 1 review. Tagger lightly touches the upper arm of a player who agrees to contact. Both stop, transfer armband, then swap roles. New tagger waits 5 seconds before walking; no immediate tag-back of previous tagger. Crossing boundary: stop and walk back in, without punishment. Teacher signal stops everyone.
## Safety and management
Walking only, look where travelling, no backward walking. No blocking, pushing, grabbing clothing, diving or deliberately surrounding someone. If crowded, teacher stops and reduces group size or enlarges area. Pain, discomfort with contact or a personal stop signal ends pursuit immediately.
## Adaptations and assessment
No-contact version: call the player's name within arm's length without reaching; resolve uncertainty by continuing play. Agree one version for the whole group. For mobility aids, teacher adapts space and a safe shared pace; no touching chairs. Easier: larger area. Harder: two taggers only with extra space and a fresh crowding check. Observe walking, spacing and fair swaps, not tag totals.
