## Setup and goal
Ages 10–15; pair, soft football, two markers in 3×7 m lane. Goal: guide diagonally outward using outside of foot without a hard kick.
## Procedure and timing
6 minutes: 1 demonstration, 4 practice and rest, 1 feedback. Dribble slowly 2 m to marker, touch gently with outside of foot and follow diagonally another 2 m. Stop and carry ball back. Three attempts per foot then swap; observer behind outside lane.
## Coaching and safety
Comfortable ankle, small touch; do not roll weight onto foot edge or force knee rotation. Distant ball: softer touch. No defender, tackle or fast sharp cut. Retrieve escaped ball after stopping.
## Adaptations and assessment
Easier: stationary ball, single touch. Harder: look at direction cue before arrival without accelerating. Hand guidance offers a different goal if kicking is inaccessible. Observe three direction changes with ball within one step; no fixed angle required.
