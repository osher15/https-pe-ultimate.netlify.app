## Setup and goal
Ages 11–17; 4 participants. Four 2×2 m movement squares with metre gaps within a 5×5 m area; timer and teacher-written action cards. Goal: lead clearly and check that others can follow.
## Procedure, rules and ending
Each leads one minute while others move in place. Choose from marching, a small side step and close, and comfortable elbow bends/extensions. Three 20-second sections per minute; leader demonstrates and names the action at each start, with demonstration included in the time. Actions may repeat. Follow with one minute of feedback/change: identify one clear instruction and one improvement. Four 2-minute turns let everyone lead. 'Stop' plus raised palm stops everyone at any time.
## Safety and adaptations
No running, jumps, contact or leaving squares; side steps stay small. Teacher supervises and remains responsible for stopping and adaptations. If unclear, stop and ask; no exercise punishment. Use cards and seated heel lifts, comfortable sideways foot taps and elbow movement. Easier: repeat one action. Harder: check understanding before changing, not faster pace.
## Timing and assessment
12 minutes: 2 preparation/demo, 8 four lead-and-feedback turns, 2 reflection. Output: a short instruction improved through feedback. Assess clarity, demonstration and listening; standalone activity, not a full warm-up or professional qualification for the young leader.
