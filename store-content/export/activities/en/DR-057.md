## Setup and goal
Ages 10–16; stable non-wheeled chair, feet reaching floor, 2×2 m space. Goal: extend and return one knee comfortably without trunk swinging.
## Procedure and timing
5 minutes: 1 demonstration, 3 practice and rest, 1 feedback. Sit comfortably upright with support as needed. Partly straighten one knee or reach comfortable range, hold two seconds, lower slowly. Four each leg, 30 seconds rest; up to two rounds.
## Coaching and safety
No forced knee locking, ankle weights or partner pressure. Thigh stays on seat. Swaying trunk: reduce range. Stop for pain or cramp; chair must stay still.
## Adaptations and assessment
Easier: smaller extension without hold. Harder: three-second lowering, no added weight. Adjust seat height and support beforehand. Observe four comfortable repetitions per side and controlled lowering; movement practice, not strength testing or rehabilitation.
