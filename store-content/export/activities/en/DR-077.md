## Setup and goal
Ages 12–17; pair and 3×3 m space. Goal: transfer weight sideways and return under control without twisting the knee.
## Procedure
From a comfortable stance, take a small side step, slightly bend the stepping knee and move hips back. Push gently through that foot and return to standing. Take 4 repetitions per side, then swap with the observer outside; up to 2 rounds.
## Safety and adaptations
Feet face comfortably forwards; no compulsory depth or forced stretch. Easier: wide stance and weight shift without stepping, beside a stable wall. Harder: pause 2 seconds before returning. Seated option: small side-to-side weight shifts without lifting the buttocks, assessed separately.
## Timing and assessment
Target: 3 of 4 repetitions per side with knee aligned with foot and a stable return. Rest during the partner's turn.
Suggested duration: 6 minutes — 1 for demonstration, 4 for turns, retrieval and rests, 1 for feedback. Start after appropriate physical preparation; stop for pain or dizziness.
