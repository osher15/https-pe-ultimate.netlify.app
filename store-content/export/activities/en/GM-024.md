## Setup and goal
Ages 10–17; 4 participants, 4 light channels about 40 cm long and at least 10 cm internal width, foam ball about 6 cm across and wide stable container. Smooth edges; 3×6 m lane, container 3 m from start. Goal: deliver through connected channels.
## Procedure, rules and ending
Hold channels below waist and join end to end on a gentle slope. Roll one ball. Once it leaves their channel, that person may walk around outside to the front and reconnect; holder of the ball-bearing channel stays still. An open palm may stop the ball for joining. Delivery without a drop earns a point. Drop: everyone stops, retrieves and restarts; points remain. Up to 3 journeys per round, then change leader and starting positions.
## Safety and adaptations
No running with channels, swinging, throwing or holding near faces. Use the clear outside route, never step over equipment. Ensure joins cannot pinch fingers. Easier: nearer container. Seated alternative: fixed channel row reaching a nearer container without moving places, a separate coordination goal. Harder: fewer planned hand stops, not speed.
## Timing and assessment
10 minutes: 2 explanation/trial, 3 round, 1 rest/change, 3 round, 1 reflection. Stop on time even if 3 journeys are incomplete. Record deliveries and coordinated stop requests, not group speed rankings.
