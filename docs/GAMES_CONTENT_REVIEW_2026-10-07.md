## B. Task and medal lines

No medal threshold is mathematically impossible from the text alone; opportunity and round length are not quantified. The concerns below are conditional, not invented success rates. The shared menu is 2–5 push-ups; 5–10 squats; 5–15 seconds plank; 5–15 seconds wall sit; 5–10 star jumps. The shared UI permits easier tasks or skipping, but g-tagdown's “must” can contradict that permission. A wall sit also requires an accessible wall not listed in most game equipment. Teacher should choose a safe side area, prevent tasks in running/throwing paths, and keep skip/easier choices effective in every rule.
