## Open questions and limitations (also in placements.csv)
1. **FB-02 Main B not drawn (exception row, like BB-05).** The text says "Trio triangle; after passing, fill the empty
   corner." Three players on a three-corner triangle leave no empty corner after a pass. Is there a fourth corner or
   cone, or does the passer move into the receiver's corner? Needs a decision before drawing.
2. FB-08 applied game says "3v3 with two gates" but also "between ball and goal". The template goal stands for the
   defended goal; the gates are not drawn. The FB-08 warm-up and Main A are not drawn (2 scenes per family were enough).
3. FB-09 Main B and the applied game mention two goals; only the template goal is drawn. FB-04 uses a two-metre goal;
   the template goal is full size. All distances are schematic.
4. Placement choices that the text does not fix (listed as open_question notes): which gate is open (FB-03-D1,
   FB-05-D1, FB-06-D2), where the end gate sits (FB-02-D2), the angle change direction (FB-02-D1), the rotation
   direction at FB-10 stations, where the central gate sits (FB-08-D1), how many players exit (FB-09-D1).
5. Observers (FB-07 Main A, FB-10 Main B) have no schema object. FB-07-D1 draws the observer as A3; FB-10-D2 does not
   draw the observer (stated in the alt text).
6. A shot has no path kind: shots are drawn as `pass` arrows to the goal mouth (as accepted in the HB-07 pilot).
7. FB-05-D1: badges 1 and 2 sit close together at the turning point of the receiver; readable but tight.
