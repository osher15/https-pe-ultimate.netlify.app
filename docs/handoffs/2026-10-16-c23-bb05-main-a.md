**BB-05-D3, round 1 shown** (template `open-area`): three cones in a wide triangle, three players 1, 2, 3 (one per cone), one ball. Cones: top-right, bottom-right, left. 3 (bottom-right) holds the ball.
1. Dashed pass from 3 to 2.
2. 3 cuts a short way to the right (the basket direction, away from the triangle).
3. Two simultaneous movements: 3 runs from the cut point to the cone 1 occupies (outer arc), and 1 runs straight down to the cone 3 left. Both carry order 3.

**BB-05-D4, round 2 shown**: starts from the positions that result from round 1 (1 now at bottom-right, 3 at top-right, 2 at left with the ball). Pass 2 to 1, 2 cuts to the right, then 2 and 3 swap cones (3 on the upper arc, 2 on the lower arc).

Why `open-area`: Main A is a cone route with no court lines, and the drill is described as a triangle of 3-4 m. `bb-half` would imply a real basket and court scale. The basket is a predefined direction of travel, so it is not drawn (the schema has no basket object); the right edge is the basket direction and every alt text says so.

Why two scenes: a single scene would need either ghost players or about 8 overlapping arrows for two rounds, and the second round starts from different positions. D3 carries "round 1 shown" in all five titles and mentions round 2 in all five alts; D4 shows round 2. The cone layout is repeated only because the drill physically requires the same three cones. Both scenes use anchor step 1 of mainA (the text has a single "Process" step); quotes are verbatim substrings of the EN export.

Titles and alts are written per scene in he, en, ar, ru, es. They are drafts and still need native review.
