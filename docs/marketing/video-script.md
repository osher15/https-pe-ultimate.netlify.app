# Demo video script (task G4) — Gemini first pass, edited by Claude 2026-10-12

Length 40-45 s, no voice-over, screen recording of the real app. Record in **demo mode, English interface** (Settings/lock screen -> Demo), phone in portrait, with the same clock trick as the screenshots (`tools/store-screenshots.js` fixes the clock at 07:30 so the timetable shows an upcoming lesson), or simply record in the morning with a timetable entry set for the next lesson. The tap sequence was checked against the app's real buttons: Start lesson, "Everyone's here", Fitness tests, Beep test Start/Pause, Classes.

| Time | Taps and navigation | On-screen text (EN) | On-screen text (HE) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00-0:05 | Open the app on **Today**. Tap **Start lesson** on the next lesson. | Run your day offline | ניהול היום, גם בלי אינטרנט |
| 0:05-0:12 | Live lesson screen opens. Tap **Everyone's here**. | Attendance in one tap | נוכחות בלחיצה אחת |
| 0:12-0:20 | Tap **Measure** (fitness tests). Scroll slowly through the test list. | 31 fitness tests | 31 מבחני כושר |

| 0:40-0:45 | Cut to the app logo. Add the Google Play badge **only after the app is live**; until then show the app name only. | Free. No account needed. | חינם. בלי הרשמה. |

## What was changed from Gemini's draft
- "30+ fitness tests & norms" -> "31 fitness tests": the app has 31 tests, and the norms wording is still waiting for the owner's decision.
- "All student data in one place" -> "Classes and results on your device": truer to the privacy promise.
- "Stop test" -> "Pause": the button in the app is Pause/Reset.
- Added the missing "Back to lesson" step between fitness tests and beep test.
- The Google Play badge is placeholder until the store page exists; Google's badge guidelines apply when it is used (verify at that time).
- Hebrew texts shortened and made consistent with the listing ("חינם. בלי הרשמה.").

## Recording notes
Mute or lower the beep sound if the video will have music. Do not show the photo-finish screen (no accuracy study). Use only invented demo pupils; never record real pupil names.
