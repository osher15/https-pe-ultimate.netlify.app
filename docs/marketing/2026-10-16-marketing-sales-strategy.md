# PE Ultimate — Marketing & Sales Plan (2026-10-16)

Status: proposal; no code, billing, merge or deployment approved.

## Pricing
Free: ₪0, 4 active classes, core tools, 20 lesson plans, 3 generated plans/month.
Pro: ₪10/month or ₪85/year; daily toolkit, reports, annual tracking and advanced exports.
Ultimate: ₪20/month or ₪169/year; multiyear progress, advanced reporting, curriculum planning and automation.
Annual savings: Pro ₪35 (29.2%); Ultimate ₪71 (29.6%) versus 12 monthly payments. Annual plans provide 12 months of access.

## Trial
21-day opt-in Ultimate trial, no credit card, no auto-charge. At expiry offer Free/Pro/Ultimate. Never delete student data or disable export. Extra classes should become read-only, not deleted. Trial cannot demonstrate real multiyear progress; synthetic demo data must be labeled.

## Marketing
Teacher-first, not GEFEN-first. Positioning: More teaching, less tapping. English and Hebrew launch messaging; Arabic, Russian and Spanish after native-speaker review. Teacher interviews (10–15), pilot (20–30), measure first class/test/report, day-7/day-21 retention and willingness to pay. Use authentic demos, not unverified accuracy/privacy/compliance claims.

Hebrew headline: PE Ultimate — יותר הוראה, פחות התעסקות בטלפון. 21 ימי ניסיון בכל כלי Ultimate, ללא כרטיס אשראי (Coming Soon until implemented).
English headline: PE Ultimate — more teaching, less tapping. 21-day Ultimate trial, no credit card (Coming Soon until implemented).

## Implementation and review gates
Audit actual features and product claims. Centralized entitlement policy; secure store purchase verification, restore, offline grace period, clock/device abuse prevention, refunds and privacy review. Preserve all grades, records, local backup/export on downgrade. No payments or official ministry integration claims without approval. No merge or deploy.
