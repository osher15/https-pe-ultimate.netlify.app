## English
**One sentence:** A free, offline field kit for PE teachers, fitness trainers and sports coaches.

**Three benefits**
- **Offline in the gym:** works without internet or mobile reception, on the field or in the hall.
- **Fast during the lesson:** run fitness tests, attendance and timers without breaking the flow.
- **You keep your data:** pupil records stay on your device; there is no account and no server of ours.

**Get the app:** Download PE Ultimate App for free and run your classes, lessons and measurements from your phone or tablet. [Google Play link — after launch]

**FAQ**
- **Is it free?** Yes. It is free, with no ads, analytics or tracking.
- **Do I need internet?** No. Everything works offline on your device.
- **Where is my data?** On your device only. It is not sent to the operator.
- **Can I move to a new phone?** Yes: save a backup file (password encryption is optional) and restore it on the new device.
- **Which devices?** Android phones and tablets.
- **How do I contact you?** Through the optional contact form inside the app (About).
